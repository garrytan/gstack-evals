Weekly primary, candidate minus stable, two-sided 0.05 at 80% power. MDD = 2.802 sqrt(d / N).
d is the share of pairs where the two arms disagree; N is the number of pairs.
  S80: 80 tasks, one session each, d 0.10: MDD 9.9 points
  S80: 80 tasks, one session each, d 0.15: MDD 12.1 points
  S80: 80 tasks, one session each, d 0.20: MDD 14.0 points
  S390: 130 tasks, three sessions each, d 0.10: MDD 4.5 points
  S390: 130 tasks, three sessions each, d 0.15: MDD 5.5 points
  S390: 130 tasks, three sessions each, d 0.20: MDD 6.3 points
S390 pairs share a task three at a time, so its interval is cluster-robust by task; under pure session noise
the clustering costs nothing, and a fixed task-by-release effect widens it.
