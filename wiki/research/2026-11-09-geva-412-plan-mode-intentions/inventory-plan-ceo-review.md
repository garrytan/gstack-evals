## 2. Claimed value, one sentence

plan-ceo-review exists to make a plan worth building before any code: it challenges whether this is the right problem, pushes the plan toward the best version of the product for its user at a scope the user approves mode by mode and item by item, and forces the plan to say how it fails in production and where it leaves the product in 6 to 12 months (SKILL.md.tmpl:6-12; SKILL.md:215-234, 598-618, 747-795; review-sections.md:144-145, 291-303).

## 4. Steps vs intention

1. The mode recommendation can push ambitious work toward reduction. 0E applies "more than 15 planned changed files: recommend SCOPE REDUCTION" before the greenfield-to-EXPANSION rule (SKILL.md:752-756). A large new product gets a reduction recommendation by file count, while the Philosophy asks for the platonic ideal (SKILL.md:217) and the triggers are "think bigger" and "expand scope" (SKILL.md:19-21). The file count is the model's own estimate (SKILL.md:752).

2. Under /autoplan the CEO ambition mostly does not run. autoplan forces SELECTIVE EXPANSION (autoplan/sections/ceo-phase.md:10), auto-approves only expansions inside the blast radius under one day of CC effort and defers the rest (ceo-phase.md:18), and accepts "reasonable" premises (ceo-phase.md:11). The platonic ideal is EXPANSION-only (review-sections.md:60), so it never runs under autoplan. autoplan's review-log line hardcodes mode SELECTIVE_EXPANSION (autoplan/SKILL.md:698). The 0E procedure (SKILL.md:747-795) is bypassed by an override.

3. The default unattended Capy run skips the CEO phase. --kind feature is the default for unattended Capy runs (lib/autoplan-lean.ts:2-6, 40-45; bin/gstack-autoplan:70-73). It runs the eng phase alone; the CEO lens becomes a six-sentence role (lib/autoplan-prompts.ts:131) plus checklist.md appended to the one reviewer's brief (lib/autoplan-lean.ts:121-125). That checklist tells the reviewer to agree scope and mode in Step 0, carry the ledger and run Sections 1-10 (checklist.md:9-15), none of whose text the reviewer receives.

4. The runner checklist names work the runner reviewer cannot do and omits outputs autoplan needs. In light runs the checklist goes into the native reviewer's prompt (lib/autoplan-prompts.ts:175, 197), whose criteria are the five strategic questions (bin/gstack-autoplan-snapshot.ts:38-45) and the plan. The checklist lists saved terminal report, review log row, next-skill handoff and outside voice as always required (checklist.md:14, 33-36), while autoplan's skip list skips the report, dashboard, chaining and outside voice (autoplan/SKILL.md:474-489). It omits Implementation Tasks entirely (compare review-sections.md:745-785, the JSONL /autoplan aggregates) and omits Approval readiness, 0H and 0I. It marks Scope Expansion Decisions "always" (checklist.md:28) though review-sections.md:727 limits it to EXPANSION and SELECTIVE.

5. Most required substance is engineering, and all of it is mandatory at every depth. Sections 1-10 run "in every depth" (SKILL.md:559-560; review-sections.md:5-9) and the priority hierarchy forbids shortening them (SKILL.md:247-251). The CEO-specific intentions (CEO-1, CEO-2, CEO-6) are a small share of required outputs, so a run can satisfy every required output with a thorough eng review and a thin premise challenge. 0A has no required shape beyond the premise-table reality row (SKILL.md:598-611).

6. Self-scored numbers. The spec reviewer's own 1-10 quality score is the recorded metric (SKILL.md:929-931, 945, 956). At the three-launch cap, "issues verified: k of N" is the model checking its own edits (SKILL.md:939). The Lake Score counts how often the user picked the 10/10 option, but the model assigns the scores and the recommendation (review-sections.md:794-799; SKILL.md:685-691). The Completion Summary is a table of counts the model fills in (review-sections.md:801-835), which rewards the number of findings over their weight.

7. Volume floors and per-item ceremony. The delight scan needs at least five items (SKILL.md:810; review-sections.md:61), which trivial items can satisfy. Every addition, cut, deferral and TODO gets its own question with a saved payload and a read-back (SKILL.md:699-731, 818, 828-833; review-sections.md:632). The procedure can be followed to the letter while the user approves by fatigue, which empties CEO-4 of substance.

8. Format gates, not substance gates. The exit gate checks the last heading, the report table, VERDICT and the final sentinel line (SKILL.md:1004-1015).
