# Product and demand

The current traveler page shows booking details, cancellation status, account details, and a support number. It already has the identity checks needed for receiving a refund. A small daily confirmation task could be released through that page in six engineer-days, including desk tooling and reporting. It does not depend on another traveler feature or a replacement account system.

Trip itineraries are emailed as a printable document. Travelers rate them well. Separate research found occasional interest in group chat and photo journals, but no relationship to cancellation complaints or repeat refund contacts. Saved station guides and points are longer-term retention ideas. No contract requires any of these areas, and operators do not need them to submit or settle a cancellation. The intended recovery cohort can use the current page.

The content team owns station text and can license it for a workspace. The discount allowance is funded. Moderation at the proposed group sizes fits existing support coverage. These facts make those features feasible, but their combined construction and testing takes most of the twelve-week release window.
