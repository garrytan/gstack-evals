# Approved resources

Three engineers are assigned for the full quarter. Their combined estimate for the workspace and refund work is 31 engineer-weeks, leaving five weeks for testing and integration across the team. There are no other committed releases in that period. The budget includes hosting, licensing, support training, and a contingency allocation. The release does not require a hiring decision.

Separately, the board has approved a revolving refund facility up to $120,000. It can advance traveler repayments against confirmed operator cancellation balances. It has never been used because the current operating procedure waits for cash receipt. A finance review found average outstanding cancelled deposits of $48,000 and a peak of $72,000. All seven operators have settled their cancelled balances in full within their contracted remittance dates. The facility changes cash timing rather than the traveler's price, and its carrying cost is covered in the standing finance budget.
