# Cancellation audit

PathMarsh reviewed 180 recent operator-cancelled bookings. In 174 cases the receiving account was correct and verified before the cancellation. All 180 travelers completed the current confirmation step within one day. Support checked the six corrected accounts and found that none added more than a day to processing. Refund requests were filed with operators on the day of cancellation or the following morning.

The delayed repayments occur because the trip operator holds the deposit and remits cancellation funds on its twice-monthly schedule. PathMarsh currently waits for that remittance before paying the traveler. In the audited cases, 83 repayments took more than seven days; 81 of those waited for an operator remittance. Operators say changing the traveler confirmation messages will not change their payment dates.

Finance can distinguish the request timestamp, operator receipt, and cleared traveler payment. The seven-day target concerns cleared money. Travelers interviewed understood the cancellation messages and knew the company had requested the money. Their complaint was paying for an absent holiday while waiting for cash to be returned.
