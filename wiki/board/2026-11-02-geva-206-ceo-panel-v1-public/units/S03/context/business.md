# Company and current work

ShelfQuay is a new venture formed by a retail operations manager and a bookkeeper. Its initial funds cover one quarter without outside investment. The founders have permission to recruit shops through a district association newsletter. The association is not a buyer or a venue operator.

The objective approved by the founders is higher net cash recovery for shops disposing of seasonal goods. Shops currently receive between 18% and 30% of original retail price from clearance buyers, after transport. The founders want an alternative that pays more after sale fees, item preparation, and shop staff time. Catalog participation itself is not a paid benefit to the shops.

Twelve shops supplied stock sheets during research. Each has between 80 and 220 eligible goods, can set prices, and has a staff member available for intake. All twelve accepted the proposed fee in a paid-reservation exercise that was refunded at the end. Shoppers in a comparable district sale bought 410 of 980 offered items. This supplies a planning range, not a sales promise.
