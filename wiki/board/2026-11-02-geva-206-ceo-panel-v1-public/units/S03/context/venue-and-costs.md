# Venue arrangements

The preferred venue is a vacant ground-floor retail unit. A refundable date hold lets ShelfQuay enter to measure shelves, but it does not allow resale events. The property manager must issue a commercial-use letter before the operator may take confirmed trader bookings or announce a public sale there. Applications require the operator's insurance certificate and trading hours. The manager's published processing time is ten working days from receipt of a complete application. There is no temporary permission or alternative approved hall.

The venue costs $700 per weekend. Two assistants cost $640, transport costs $180, and card fees run at 2.1% of sales. At twelve merchants and the conservative sales range, weekend receipts cover those recurring costs. The coordinator's quarter salary and setup work are included in the funded launch budget. Insurance is quoted and can start within one day. Checkout rental and the payment account are already available.
