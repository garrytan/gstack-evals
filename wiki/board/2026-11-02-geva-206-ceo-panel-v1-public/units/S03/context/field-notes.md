# Merchant observations

Nine of twelve owners said that a stock list is easy to produce because their till software already exports one. Eight said they would list the same unsold batch repeatedly if there were no sale. Most goods occupy storage rather than selling space, so moving them onto a website does not release meaningful shop capacity.

Owners evaluate clearance channels by the cash deposited after fees and handling costs. One shop's earlier online sale listed 240 items and sold eleven. Another listed only 70 items but sold 58 at a higher net return. Both owners offered these examples when asked how they choose a channel. They also care about accurate statements and not losing goods during shared intake.

Shoppers liked inspecting fragile homewares in person. Their main requests were clear prices, condition labels, and a single checkout. They did not ask for shipping or advance reservations. The district newsletter can reach 6,400 nearby households without paid advertising.
