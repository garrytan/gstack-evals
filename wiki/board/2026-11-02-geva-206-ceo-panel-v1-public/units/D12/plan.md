# Rivetfen guild enrollment repair

## The shortfall
Rivetfen sells an established safety-briefing service to small fabrication employers. The service is retaining customers, but the direct-email campaign is reaching too few authorized employer contacts. We will add guild-based enrollment to repair that acquisition shortfall. This release changes access to the existing offer, not the briefing content, service promise or price.

The twelve-week goal is 240 new qualified employer registrations within a $22,000 campaign and delivery budget. A qualified registration identifies a real member employer, names a person authorized to evaluate training purchases and includes consent for follow-up. We will not count individual workers, duplicate companies or unverified email addresses toward that goal.

## The enrollment service
Each guild chapter will receive a branded link to a short employer-registration page. It will explain the existing $600 annual briefing package and let an authorized contact request a demonstration or proceed to the normal purchase page. The guild will verify membership; Rivetfen will verify company uniqueness and purchasing role. The guild will not buy subscriptions on members' behalf.

The system will return registration counts to the chapter without sharing member responses or course records. Contacts can withdraw from follow-up at any time. Employers who buy will use the existing onboarding flow and receive the standard service. We will not create chapter-specific course variants or promise new forms of instruction.

## Opening and extending the channel
We will open two chapters first. Once sixty qualified employers have registered and the contact-consent checks pass, we will open the same enrollment service in six more chapters. This step will follow the registration result rather than wait for those employers to finish their annual training cycle.

All eight chapters use the same offer, contact rules and purchase agreement. We will stop enrollment when the total reaches 240 or the twelve-week window closes. No chapter will be added beyond those eight during this campaign. A chapter can remove its link immediately, and registrations already received remain subject to the contact's consent.

## Work and staffing
One engineer has three weeks to add chapter links, verification status and the registration report. The customer team will use the existing purchase and onboarding system. Week four will rehearse the complete process with each of the two opening chapters. The remaining eight weeks cover enrollment, approved follow-up and reporting.

The campaign manager has four hours a week reserved for verification exceptions. Existing onboarding staff can handle thirty new employers weekly. Registration alone creates no service obligation or training booking. We will keep paid purchases within existing instructor and support capacity rather than reserve new staff on the strength of contact volume.

## Review after twelve weeks
We will report verified reach and cost per qualified employer as the channel test result. The 240-registration target must fit the $22,000 ceiling and every counted contact must meet the role and consent rules. Demonstrations, paid contracts and renewals will be reported separately so the commercial team can estimate the next acquisition budget.

The next campaign will require a fresh budget decision. This release does not authorize a national program or a permanent guild subsidy. Any service-quality incident affecting new buyers will use the existing pause process, and no registration threshold can override it. At campaign end, inactive contacts will be removed under the current retention policy, while paid employers continue on their normal annual terms.
