# Existing service evidence
The current annual briefing package serves 620 small fabrication employers. Annual renewal is 93%. Required briefing completion is 96%, customer audits have accepted the course evidence for two years and service-related refund rates remain below 1%. Those results include employers from all eight proposed guild chapters, although most reached Rivetfen through older local introductions.

The new campaign does not change course content, employer duties or evidence format. The chapter population has the same trade mix, staff sizes and training requirements as current customers. No new accreditation or curriculum decision is outstanding.

The $600 annual price covers instruction, materials and support at the intended usage. Existing staff can onboard thirty employers per week. Ordinary service-quality stop rules apply equally to buyers from a guild link and buyers from other channels.
