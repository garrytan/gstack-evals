# Chapter agreements
Eight fabrication guild chapters have signed short campaign agreements. Each will place the same invitation in its member bulletin and purchasing portal. Each can verify employer membership and permits a vendor introduction to a named purchasing contact. They will receive aggregate registration counts only. They take no referral commission and require no annual prepayment.

The two opening chapters have 130 eligible employers not using Rivetfen. The remaining six have 510. In a small earlier contact exercise, forty-one of fifty participating member firms supplied a valid purchasing contact through a guild introduction. Direct vendor email reached seven of the same fifty firms.

All eight chapters follow the same purchasing process. Employers can buy the standard monthly-start annual package directly. Neither a guild committee vote nor a separate adviser referral is required once an authorized employer contact chooses to proceed.
