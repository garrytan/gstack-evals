# Approved channel experiment
Rivetfen's board approved a bounded test of access to authorized employer contacts. Direct emails yielded four qualified contacts per month against a target of twenty. Interviews with nonresponders found that guild filtering kept most unfamiliar vendor mail out of the purchasing inbox. The service itself was not evaluated by those firms.

The approved experiment asks whether a guild introduction can reach 240 distinct authorized employers in twelve weeks at no more than $22,000. The first two chapters may extend to six named additional chapters after sixty valid registrations. This remains one capped channel test; it does not authorize a larger service rollout or continuing subsidy.

Training outcomes and paid conversion will be reported, but the question assigned to this release is channel reach. The offer and its delivery model have already been evaluated separately.
