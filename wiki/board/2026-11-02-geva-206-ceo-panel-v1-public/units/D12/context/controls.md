# Resources and exposure
The campaign budget includes one engineer for three weeks, verification work, chapter materials and contingency. Registration processing uses the existing contact database, with a new chapter field and verification status. The scoped changes fit the engineer's estimate without altering course systems.

The campaign manager has four hours per week for exceptions. The earlier exercise took eight minutes per qualified employer, within the reserved hours over twelve weeks. Employers can purchase through a tested self-service flow or request an existing sales appointment. Registration is neither an enrollment in training nor a promise of a reserved delivery date.

The campaign cap is 240 new employer contacts. Additional chapters cannot be opened once the cap is reached. A separate approval is needed for any later campaign. Consent checks, membership validation and duplicate removal must pass before counts can trigger additional chapter links.
