# Paid-use cover
The halls' pooled insurance currently covers internal community programs. Whether the same policy can cover third-party paid rehearsal hire has not been determined. The insurer has not declined the use, but has not accepted it either. Cover may require different hire terms, supervision arrangements or a premium that changes the offer's costs.

The parent organization cannot host paid external rehearsals until the insurer gives written cover. This condition applies at every time of day, including evenings and weekends outside the youth allocation. The existing internal-program cover cannot be used as an interim arrangement.

The insurer offers a six-working-day eligibility assessment from a sample hire agreement, room list and description of use. It needs no working booking software. That assessment can begin this week without charge. Management identifies this as the largest unresolved question for launching paid rehearsal hire.
