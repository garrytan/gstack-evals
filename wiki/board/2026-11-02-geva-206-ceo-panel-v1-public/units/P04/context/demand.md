# Rehearsal interviews
Twenty-four tutors and group directors described regular demand for affordable neighborhood rooms. Most can use either later weekday evenings or weekend mornings; a smaller set also likes the end of the working day. They will pay $48 per hour for the listed room sizes and existing instruments. None needs a public-performance license for these private rehearsals.

The two music-teacher associations will distribute a booking invitation after the service is approved. Buyers have authority to make direct room-hire purchases. Sample room descriptions, cancellation terms and access instructions were reviewed successfully with six customers.

The halls already manage keys and physical access. Paid rehearsal bookings would use the same staff coverage as current internal activities. The first sixty weekly hours fit available staff time without an additional hire, and no customer requires an overnight response promise.
