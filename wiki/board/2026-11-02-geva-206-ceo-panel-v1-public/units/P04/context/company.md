# Alderwisp priorities
Alderwisp Rooms manages twelve neighborhood arts halls under community-use agreements. Its board approved a paid rehearsal line to improve room income, subject to a ranked operating rule: preserve the free youth-program allocation first, then earn income from time outside that allocation. Income is not a reason to reduce the protected hours.

Every weekday from 3 p.m. to 7 p.m. is reserved for the youth program at all twelve halls. The promise covers the coming school year, including currently unfilled places. The allocation cannot be reassigned to commercial customers before the next annual program review. Hall managers have no individual authority to waive it.

Rooms are available for commercial rehearsal from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. on weekdays and during published weekend blocks, subject to the ordinary hire approval. The new line can earn income within those hours without changing the youth agreement.
