# Quietspan paid rehearsal rooms

## New business brief
Quietspan will open a booking service for paid rehearsals in neighborhood arts halls. Professional music tutors, choir directors and small performance groups will be able to reserve a room, receive access instructions and pay through one account. We will begin with twelve halls managed by our parent organization, Alderwisp Rooms.

The objective is sixty paid room-hours a week by the end of the first operating quarter, with positive contribution after host payments and incident handling. The launch is limited to rehearsal use without a public audience. We will not sell event tickets, serve food or permit amplified late-night performances.

## The opening inventory
We will sell weekday blocks from 4 p.m. to 7 p.m. in each hall as the standard opening inventory. Those blocks align with the end of the working day and allow a tutor to book a recurring weekly series. We will hold back later evenings and weekends from this release so the service can run on one simple timetable.

Customers can buy a single hour or a four-week series at the same hourly rate. The system will show room size, piano availability and permitted activities. It will not auction slots or offer paid priority over an existing booking. Hall managers will receive the final weekly list and handle physical access under their current opening procedures.

## Customer service
The price will be $48 per hour, with $30 paid to the hall and a $3 allowance for payment costs and incident handling. The remaining $15 contributes to fixed operations. A customer can cancel more than forty-eight hours ahead without charge. Hall closures receive an automatic refund and an offer of another date.

We will recruit through music-teacher associations and existing hall mailing permissions. All twelve halls are within the same two districts, and buyers can purchase directly under the standard room-hire agreement. We will offer a staffed weekday contact line within the hall team's current coverage. Outside those hours, customers can submit a nonurgent request for the next working day.

## Building the service
Three engineers have nine weeks allocated within a funded $104,000 startup budget. Weeks one through three cover room listings, availability and recurring reservations. Weeks four through six add payment, refunds and the hall-manager schedule. Weeks seven and eight complete customer messaging, incident records and operating rehearsals.

Week nine will be the insurance eligibility exercise for commercial rehearsal use. We will send the finished service and sample customer agreement to the halls' pooled insurer, run a paid-use scenario with its team and incorporate its response before accepting public bookings. The final release checklist requires written cover for the proposed use.

## Launch limits
We will cap the first quarter at twelve halls and sixty paid hours each week. Fixed operating costs are $30,000 a year, and the parent organization has separately funded the startup build. We will not spend customer balances on development or sign new hall guarantees during the pilot.

The weekly review will record booked and used hours, net contribution, cancellations, hall-manager workload and any displacement of existing hall activities. Growth requires positive contribution, at least 90% of booked hours used and no unagreed cancellation of another activity. A hall can suspend future listings immediately while a service issue is resolved. Any expansion to other times or more halls requires a new inventory and operating review.
