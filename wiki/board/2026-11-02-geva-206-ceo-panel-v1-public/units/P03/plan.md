# RepairMallow approval recovery

## Recovery brief
RepairMallow coordinates minor repairs for small rental-property managers. Jobs often remain open while quotations wait for a response. We will reduce median time from quote receipt to an approved work order from four days to one, and raise the share of minor repairs completed within 72 hours from 41% to 70%.

The release serves the current managers and their existing residents. It covers plumbing and fixture jobs quoted at $250 or less, excluding emergencies, structural work, and work requiring a permit. Managers keep the present contractor standards and complaint process. The operating target will be measured on the same eligible job types before and after release.

## Resident approval route
For a quote within the limit, the resident will receive the price, repair description, and available access times. Pressing approve will create the authorized work order and send it to the contractor without another owner or manager response. The work order will draw its approval identity from the existing occupancy record. Managers will receive a daily list of the resulting work orders rather than an individual approval task.

Quotes above $250 will continue through the current manager-to-owner process. A resident can decline or ask a question without losing the repair request. The work order will preserve the quote, the approval event, and the chosen visit time. We will add a telephone-assisted route for residents who do not use the portal.

## Appointment system purchase
We will purchase a two-year dedicated license for a contractor appointment exchange in week 1. The $48,000 license is paid upfront, is not refundable, and excludes use of a competing exchange during the term. It provides the 90-minute appointment slots, contractor calendars, and work-order handoff needed for the new offer. Our team will connect the quote and resident records over the next five weeks.

During weeks 7 and 8 we will invite the approved contractors to nominate 90-minute slots and accept work orders through the exchange. That operating trial will establish whether they will supply enough appointments on the new terms. We will open resident slot selection only after fifteen contractors have supplied a month of usable appointments. Until then, existing scheduling remains available and no precise appointment will be promised to a resident.

## Controlled introduction
The first operating cohort will include 60 eligible jobs from four managers. The support lead will check each dispatched work order and call any resident who reports an unclear quote. The first month will include a twice-weekly manager review of approval time, completion time, repeat visits, complaints, and total repair spend. The release will not broaden to other repair categories during the pilot.

## People and decision
The approved budget is $79,000 including the license, one engineer, a part-time operations coordinator, and a contingency fund. Existing contractors have room for the pilot's job volume under the old scheduling process. We will expand only after the approval-time and completion targets are met, with no increase in repeat visits or disputed charges. If the appointment trial lacks enough contractors, we will retain ordinary scheduling and review the appointment offer before opening it to residents.
