# Property agreements

All four intended pilot managers use the same owner agreement. Every non-emergency repair requires owner approval unless the owner signs a separate standing spending mandate. No owner in the proposed pilot has signed that mandate. Managers may collect quotes and arrange access, but cannot treat a resident's consent as approval to spend the owner's money. The contractor terms require an authorized owner or manager work order before work starts.

Resident lease signatures cover occupation, access arrangements, and reporting defects. They confer no delegated repair budget, including below $250. Emergency safety work has a separate rule and is outside the proposed cohort. The legal operations lead checked these terms against all sixty candidate jobs and found no existing spending delegation.

The owners are reachable through the current manager relationships. A standing budget mandate can be signed electronically without changing the lease or contractor agreement. Managers can also continue seeking individual approval. Owners have not been asked to delegate approval to residents, and no product field currently stores such authority.
