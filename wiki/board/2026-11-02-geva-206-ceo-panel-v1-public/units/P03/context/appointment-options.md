# Contractor scheduling evidence

Contractors currently offer half-day arrival windows. Residents want narrower appointments and would choose them if available. The open commercial question is whether contractors will commit enough 90-minute slots at the current repair rates. Five contractors expressed interest, twelve declined to answer without trying it, and none has committed a month of supply. The current contractor pool has enough total capacity for the pilot under ordinary half-day scheduling.

The exchange vendor offers a two-week, nonexclusive evaluation account for $600. The operations coordinator can load ten actual jobs manually, and contractors can nominate slots without any product integration. This tests the same slot duration and work-order terms as the dedicated license. Contractors have agreed to participate in that small trial. The $48,000 price is available for the next three months, so taking the evaluation does not lose a discount or reserved capacity.

An internal investment note identifies contractor acceptance of narrow slots as the main uncertainty in purchasing the exchange. Existing scheduling can remain live throughout either trial. The dedicated license is an access right to software, not a supplier guarantee of appointments, and is nonrefundable even if contractors choose not to use it.
