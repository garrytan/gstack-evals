# Current work and delays

RepairMallow serves 27 small property managers. The product records resident requests, obtains quotes from approved contractors, asks the manager for authorization, and tracks completion. Recent data covers 420 non-emergency plumbing and fixture jobs under $250. Median quote-to-authorization time is four days. Once properly authorized, contractors usually finish within two days, subject to resident access.

The delay is concentrated in owner approval. Managers send an email to each property owner, who controls the spending account. Owners often reply only after a weekly review of their properties. Managers would like a standing delegated budget and have asked the product team to help obtain owner instructions. Current agreements do not contain such a delegation.

Residents respond to portal messages within six hours on average, including access-time confirmations. They are comfortable reading a quote and choosing an access time. The occupancy record establishes who lives at the property and whom to contact. It is not a spending authorization or a record of the owner's agent.
