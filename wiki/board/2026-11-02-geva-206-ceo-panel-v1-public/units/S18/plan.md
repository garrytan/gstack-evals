# Mossline renewal recovery

## This quarter's job
Mossline provides route scheduling for local window-cleaning businesses. We will address the rise in employer-account cancellations with a lightweight Workday membership. The quarterly target is to recover twelve of the twenty employer accounts that left during the past year. We will preserve the core subscription and introduce the new membership alongside it.

The release has a $64,000 budget and six weeks of engineering time. We will keep prices for existing company accounts unchanged and maintain their current support coverage. We are not changing invoice collection, cleaning prices or customer ownership terms.

## Who gets Workday
Workday will serve sole-trader window cleaners who own one route and do all of its cleaning themselves. The offer is a $32 monthly personal subscription, with a daily stop list, weather-related rescheduling and a record of customer preferences. We will reserve all launch invitations for this group. Businesses with employed crews will stay on the current company edition and will not receive Workday in the renewal program.

The personal account will have no dispatcher seat or combined view of several crews. It will let one route owner prepare the next day in ten minutes and send a single rescheduling message to affected customers. A cleaner can print the route and work offline. The existing company edition will remain available for firms that want multiple workers under one account.

## Returning a working route
The campaign will direct route owners to a self-service signup and file import. We will offer a short video and email help for file-format questions, but no scheduled transition calls, bureau handover service or customer-call cutover coordination. A paid membership will begin when the owner uploads the current route book.

The campaign will use the existing mailing permissions held by our trade-association partner. We will invite sixty eligible owners in two waves. Each owner will receive a thirty-day trial and retain a downloadable route file if they leave. We will not require annual payment or exclusive use of Mossline.

## Build and support
Two engineers will use the existing stop-list and customer-message components. Weeks one and two simplify the account setup and daily route view. Weeks three and four add preference records, weather moves and the printable schedule. Week five covers import testing with sample data. Week six trains the support team and opens the first wave.

The support lead can cover sixty trials within present staffing. The new membership will not replace any existing employer support hours. We will process route files under the current customer agreement, keep a dated import history and let account owners delete their files after export. No new external service contract is required.

## Checking progress
The recovery owner will report the twenty departed employer accounts as a fixed cohort. We will track restored subscriptions, recovered annual revenue and the reasons for declining to return. The goal remains twelve employer reactivations rather than a larger count of small subscriptions. Workday activation and paid conversion will appear separately so the two customer groups remain visible.

We will not expand paid recruitment beyond the two invitation waves until the quarter closes. Continued investment requires the employer-reactivation target, no loss of support coverage for existing firms and at least forty Workday users completing four weekly schedules. If the thresholds are missed, we will keep serving paid members through their monthly terms and return the next budget decision to the product group.
