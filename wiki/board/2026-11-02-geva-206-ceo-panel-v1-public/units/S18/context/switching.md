# Route-bureau transitions
The sixty invited route owners use Clearthicket Bureau to hold their live route books and answer customer scheduling calls. Owners are free to leave. Their bureau agreement requires a jointly scheduled handover: the bureau releases the current book, the new service confirms a cutover date, and the customer telephone service is redirected. No customer file is released through a personal download link before this handover.

Clearthicket offers a two-day transfer appointment with an incoming provider. It charges $45, already allowed for in Mossline's customer-acquisition budget. The same process applies to returning employers and sole traders. Mossline's operations lead is authorized to sign the transfer form.

In the last reactivation campaign, all twenty-seven self-service invitations stalled before an active route was loaded. Twelve of those owners later activated after an account manager organized the handover. None activated from a video or file-format email alone.
