# Recovery conversations
Sixteen of the twenty departed employer owners described their return decision. Eleven want less effort coordinating weather moves and absence cover across their crews. Five would return with the current functions if dispatcher training is included. All say the purchasing decision rests with the owner or dispatcher, not an individual cleaner.

The current company edition already supports multi-crew moves. Employers use it unevenly, and account managers have begun scheduled training for firms approaching renewal. That work is funded and continues independently of the next release. The company has not promised to replace dispatchers or reduce wages.

Sole traders value a short daily plan, a printable backup and customer preference notes. A $32 price covers the support and message costs at the expected volume. The proposed sixty-owner trial list contains only opted-in contacts, all of whom operate through a route bureau today.
