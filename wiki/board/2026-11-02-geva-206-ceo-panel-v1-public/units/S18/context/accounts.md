# Account mix
Mossline has eighty-six employer accounts with five to fifteen cleaners each and 310 sole-trader accounts. Employer renewals fell to 82% this year; sole-trader renewals remain at 96%. Employer accounts produce 79% of recurring revenue. The board's current recovery objective is twelve reactivations from a fixed list of twenty employers that left during the past year. All twenty moved their live scheduling to Clearthicket Bureau and need a new handover to return.

The two groups use the product differently. Company dispatchers allocate work, supervise weather changes across crews and cover absences. Sole traders prepare one route for themselves. A personal stop list is useful to sole traders, but it is not used by company dispatchers as their management workspace.

Every employer cancellation interview was with an owner or dispatcher. None proposed moving to a personal membership. The sole-trader survey showed interest in a simpler daily view, but that segment has not experienced a retention decline.
