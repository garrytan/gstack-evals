# Quarter capacity
Two engineers have six weeks assigned and can reuse the existing stop-list, offline print and customer-message modules. Product estimates put the new personal edition within the $64,000 allocation. The support team has room for sixty trials as well as the employer training already booked.

Trade-association invitations can reach both employer owners and sole traders, with their consent. The association does not perform bureau handovers. Mossline can offer the transfer service itself without a new contract, hire or data-sharing approval. Its operations lead has four hours a week reserved for transitions.

The finance team can report employer reactivation and recovered revenue as a fixed cohort. Workday subscriptions can be recorded separately. The company has no requirement to move existing employers to another plan, and it can retain both editions without maintaining a second scheduling engine.
