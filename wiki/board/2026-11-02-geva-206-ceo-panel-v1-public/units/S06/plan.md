# Lanternelm member product

## Purpose for the new business
Lanternelm currently sells back-office assistance to small arts charities. We will establish a software subscription that improves annual member renewal from 64 percent to 80 percent. The new service will help a development director turn fundraising event photographs into a polished supporter gallery. This is a separate product line, sold alongside the assistance membership rather than replacing it.

## The first offer
Event Gallery will collect photographs from volunteers, let staff select approved pictures and send a branded gallery to supporters. Staff can remove a photograph after publication, set a closing date and export the approved set. Supporters will not need accounts. Each charity gets three events per year for $240 annually, including storage and normal support.

We will direct the first release to the 110 existing member charities that run at least one fundraising event each year. Account managers will demonstrate it during their normal quarterly calls. Purchasing stays with the development director who already controls the membership budget. The product will not include grant reporting, funding calendars or submission support. The account team will continue its existing services unchanged.

## Software investment
We will build a new subscription ledger, annual invoice generator, renewal runner and cancellation-credit calculator for the software business. These will be written as a fresh billing library owned by the product team and used by this and all later software offers. That library is the first four months of the six-month schedule. The final two months add charity accounts, photograph selection, access controls and publishing administration, using the existing event-rendering service.

We will use existing storage infrastructure and the current design system. No face recognition, automated image editing or social network publishing is included. Volunteer upload links will expire after fourteen days. A staff approver must publish each gallery, and photographs will be removed after the charity's chosen retention period.

## Commercial release
Thirty members have agreed to a paid first-year order for this exact offer, subject to the stated functions being available. We will release to those thirty in month six, with staff training and a staffed support desk during their event weeks. A second group can join in month eight after the first group's galleries have been delivered successfully.

## How we will judge it
The main measure is renewal among the charities offered the product compared with a matched group on the existing service. We will also record paid gallery adoption, staff time per event and supporter access failures. We will require an eight-point renewal improvement and positive contribution after support costs before hiring a dedicated sales team. Refunds are available if an event cannot be published within the agreed window. The approved six-month budget is $310,000, and the team will stop at that amount rather than borrow from member service delivery.
