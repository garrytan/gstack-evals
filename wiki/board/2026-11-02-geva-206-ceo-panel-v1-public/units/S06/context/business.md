# Operating envelope

The company has approved $310,000 and two engineers for six months. The product designer is available half-time. This funding is separate from the service staff budget. The new subscription has a positive annual contribution at the quoted price, including event-week support, storage, account management and a refund allowance. No revenue from future grant services is included in that calculation.

Account managers can reach every proposed launch customer during existing calls. The first thirty event dates fall after the six-month delivery date. Customers can continue sharing folder links until the new service arrives, and the quote contains no early delivery promise. Existing assistance subscriptions will remain available on the same terms.

The renewal comparison will match charity size, event frequency and prior membership tenure. A research contractor has reserved time to collect both renewal decisions and the reasons behind them. The board can stop further software funding without changing the current service contracts.
