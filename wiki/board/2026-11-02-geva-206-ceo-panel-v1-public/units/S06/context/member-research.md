# Member renewal review

Lanternelm supports 160 small arts charities. Membership includes bookkeeping assistance and donor administration, but not preparation of grant outcome reports. Forty members left last year. Exit interviews reached 33 of them: 27 named missed grant-report deadlines or difficulty assembling funder evidence as their reason for leaving. None attributed departure to event follow-up materials.

In the annual survey, 71 members requested easier photograph sharing after events. When asked to allocate a fixed improvement budget, 59 of those 71 put grant-report assembly first. Development directors described photograph sharing as a pleasant time saving, usually handled with a folder link in under an hour. Grant outcome reports take a median of eighteen staff hours and hold up renewal of substantial funding.

Thirty members accepted an annual $240 Event Gallery quote. All thirty said they would buy it as a small convenience. Twenty-four also said that, without grant-report assistance, their decision about renewing the broader Lanternelm membership would remain unchanged. Lanternelm's board has made membership renewal the purpose of its first software investment, rather than creating stand-alone event revenue.
