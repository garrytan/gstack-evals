# Existing records and access

The centers can report time from complete referral to confirmed booking, staff follow-up minutes and unresolved booking questions. The last eight weeks form the baseline. These are administrative totals, without hearing-test results or treatment notes.

All three centers can participate through the normal office-account route without changing clinical services. Appointment supply is sufficient for the intended first group. No additional clinician hiring or equipment is required. Patients can continue to book through their referring office without using the new service.

The company's existing subscription tools can handle either office or personal accounts. The four-week estimate covers the limited status and allocation work plus staff practice. The invitation budget is small and can be stopped immediately; wider acquisition requires a separate decision.
