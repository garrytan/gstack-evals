# New line approval

JuniperKettle is considering referral-management software for community hearing assessments. Three assessment centers joined discovery. Their main administrative cost is repeated contact between the referring office and the center to complete bookings. The business target is six paying centers, not personal subscription revenue.

The centers have budget for a shared referral-office account that tracks complete referrals and confirms an authorized appointment. All three have offered to test that office service. They already provide patients with a free appointment status phone line and ordinary rescheduling support.

The company approved a four-week, $22,000 first release and a capped group of 100 accounts. An engineer and service lead are available. There is no requirement to buy an annual supplier package or enter another region.
