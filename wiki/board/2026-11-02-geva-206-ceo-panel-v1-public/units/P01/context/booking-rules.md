# Who controls the booking

In this district, referring offices buy blocks of assessment appointments from centers. Only the referring office's coordinator can allocate one of those appointments or transfer a referral to a different center. Patients can state preferences but cannot accept a block appointment or move a referral between centers themselves.

Centers will connect their booking allocation records only to an account purchased and controlled by a referring office or center. They will not permit a personally purchased patient account to allocate appointments. A patient can still call the free status line or ask the referring office for a change.

Interviews covered 14 patients and eight referral coordinators. Patients wanted fewer calls but none would buy a separate status subscription. Coordinators wanted to pay for a shared work queue and hold the necessary authority. The centers' software budgets can fund that offer through ordinary invoicing.
