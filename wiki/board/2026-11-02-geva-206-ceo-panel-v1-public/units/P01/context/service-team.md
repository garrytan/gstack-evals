# Specialist schedule

The assigned specialists each work Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00. They share one local time zone with all three centers. They have no paid evening, overnight or weekend hours and have not accepted an on-call arrangement.

At the proposed cap, historical center data suggests 85 scheduling questions per week. Mean handling time is twelve minutes, so the total work fits comfortably within the team's weekday capacity. About a quarter of questions arrive during evenings or weekends.

The approved budget covers current hours only. There is no external answering service and no overtime reserve. Centers ordinarily promise a next-business-day response for scheduling questions. Clinical questions remain with the centers and do not enter the booking specialists' work.
