# ReedCove referral service

## First customers

ReedCove will be a new web service for arranging community hearing assessments. Its business goal is to sell a referral-management subscription to six assessment centers and reduce the time their staff spend chasing incomplete bookings.

We will start with individual patients as the paying account holders. A patient will buy an $8 monthly account, choose an assessment center and move a referral into an available appointment. Patients will confirm any change of center themselves. We will invite people through local community newsletters and bill the individual, not the referring office or assessment center.

## The first offer

The account will show the referral's current booking step, available times and a place to ask for help. It will not assess symptoms or recommend treatment. Existing assessment centers will continue to perform the clinical work under their current arrangements.

The first release will serve the three centers that participated in discovery. It will cover referral status, appointment selection and an activity history. We will not add payments for assessments, transport services or new assessment providers.

## Human help

Every account will include a human booking specialist who replies within fifteen minutes at any hour, every day. We will publish that service standard in the account offer and welcome messages. Specialists will resolve scheduling questions and pass clinical questions back to the assessment center.

The two specialists already assigned to this line will cover the service using their current shifts. The launch budget will retain their existing hours. We will not add on-call work or an outside answering service. The service lead will review each day's conversations and prepare the next day's handover.

## Four-week release

One engineer and a service lead have four weeks reserved. In the first two weeks they will connect status updates and appointment selection. The following two weeks cover specialist practice and invitation delivery. We will use existing account billing and messaging services.

The approved budget is $22,000 plus the assigned specialists' existing pay. We will cap the first group at 100 accounts. There is no long-term distribution contract, and invitations can stop when the cap is reached. Staff have confirmed the expected handling time for that volume.

## Review

We will measure time from a complete referral to a confirmed appointment, staff minutes spent on booking follow-up and paid assessment-center subscriptions. Results will be compared with the centers' previous eight weeks. The service lead will also record unresolved scheduling questions.

The initial service stays within the three centers. Expansion requires faster confirmed bookings, fewer staff follow-up minutes and a paying center willing to renew. Patients can cancel the subscription at any time, and their assessment remains available through the centers' existing office route.
