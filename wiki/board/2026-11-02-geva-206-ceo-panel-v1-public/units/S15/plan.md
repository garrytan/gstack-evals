# Stageharbor portable crew records

## The offer
Stageharbor schedules casual crews for independent performance venues. We will add a portable record of completed safety training and practical assessments. A worker can share a current record with another venue rather than ask a former manager to resend certificates. The recipient sees the issuer, course date and renewal deadline in one place.

This is a paid extension to the existing scheduling product. We want $3,000 in new monthly recurring revenue by the end of the second full quarter, while preserving venue renewals. Records will remain downloadable when a subscription ends. We will not become a training provider or issue qualifications ourselves.

## The subscriber
The first paid offer will be an $18 monthly membership purchased by freelance stagehands for their own records. We will invite two hundred freelancers who already appear on venue rosters. Each membership will include unlimited sharing with venues and storage for twenty credentials. Venues will receive shared records free but will not have an option to buy the extension for a roster or pay for worker memberships.

Marketing will emphasize personal portability between engagements. Existing venue administrators have agreed to distribute invitations to their crews, and freelancers can sign up without moving their scheduling accounts. Billing will be separate from the venue's subscription. We will offer the same monthly price to all invited workers rather than add tiers during the first release.

## The record and its use
A worker can import a certificate, identify its issuer and ask the issuer to confirm its details. The first version covers the six course families represented in the launch venues. A venue can save a dated copy to its own staffing record. Sharing is consent-based, and a worker can revoke future access without altering a venue's historical engagement record.

The support team will check incomplete issuer details but will not judge whether a worker is fit for an assignment. Venue managers retain responsibility for crew selection. Expired records will remain visible as expired rather than disappear. We will keep the existing schedule and local certificate attachments throughout the release.

## Delivery sequence
Three engineers and a product lead have ten weeks assigned. Weeks one through three deliver the worker record and import service. Weeks four through six add issuer confirmation and venue sharing. Weeks seven and eight complete subscription handling, exports and staff training.

In week nine we will send finished shared records to the three regional credential councils and run the recognition exercise with six venue hiring managers. Week ten will incorporate the responses and open paid memberships. All customer-facing work will be complete before the recognition exercise starts, so the team can present the final service in those meetings.

## Release control
The approved budget is $118,000 including twelve weeks of support. There are no annual vendor commitments. Council feedback will determine which course families may be displayed as accepted at launch; records without that status will not be sold as portable credentials. We will keep the cohort at two hundred until at least one hundred have used a shared record for a second engagement.

We will report paid recurring revenue, successful second-venue uses, refund requests and support minutes. Growth requires the revenue target and at least 90% recipient acceptance of shared records. We will stop selling a course family if its council withdraws acceptance, while preserving customers' downloadable originals.
