# Existing customers
Stageharbor sells scheduling software to sixty independent performance venues. The paying account belongs to each venue's operations manager. Casual workers use the schedule free. The extension objective is at least $3,000 of monthly recurring revenue from a useful new offer without weakening existing renewals.

Venue managers lose time confirming credentials when a worker arrives from another venue. Twelve interviewed managers want one verified roster view. Ten said they could approve a $150 monthly roster add-on from their training budget. The current purchasing agreement permits this extension and can be signed during a normal account review.

Current local certificate attachments work for repeat engagements at the same venue. They do not provide verified sharing across venues. No existing Stageharbor function offers the proposed portable record.
