# Freelance crew interviews
Researchers spoke with thirty-six freelancers across the proposed cohort. Thirty-two had recently resent a certificate to a venue, usually by email. They liked the idea of a reusable verified record. All thirty-six said training administration is paid by the hiring venue or a regional training fund. None would buy an $18 monthly personal membership, and none had reimbursement authority for such a subscription.

The training fund has no individual reimbursement scheme. It purchases courses and administration through venues. Workers said they would use a free record supplied by a venue. They do not control the venue's software or training budget.

Venue managers can invite every worker on their rosters through existing contact permissions. Email access and identity matching were tested successfully in the research sessions. There is no barrier to workers activating a record once a venue purchases access.
