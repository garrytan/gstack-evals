# Capacity and limits
The team has three engineers, a product lead and a part-time support specialist for ten weeks. The $118,000 allocation covers this release and the first twelve weeks of operation. The estimated work fits that staffing if the six course families use the same record structure. Existing issuer contacts cover the proposed cohort.

Worker consent, historical venue records and credential expiry rules have been agreed with the launch venues. Customers can retain the existing attachment workflow during a trial. No course content will be republished, and Stageharbor will not describe itself as an accrediting body.

At the proposed prices, either a venue-funded or an individual-funded model has positive contribution at sufficient volume. The commercial research separates who benefits from portability from who has a budget to buy administration. The councils' recognition review remains the largest unresolved question about whether the service can deliver portable acceptance.
