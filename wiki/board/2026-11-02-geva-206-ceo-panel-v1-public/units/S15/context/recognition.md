# Recognition work
Portable records must be recognized by the three regional credential councils before venues can rely on them instead of contacting the issuer. The councils have confirmed that the six proposed course families fall within their remit. They have not yet decided whether the proposed issuer confirmation and shared record satisfy their evidence standard.

Hiring managers say recognition is the deciding condition for using the record across venues. A record that still requires a separate issuer call would not save the expected work. This applies whether the subscription is bought by a venue or an individual.

Each council offers a ten-working-day review of a sample record and its confirmation process. A PDF sample and an account of the issuer check are sufficient; no functioning software is required. Reviews are free, and all three councils have slots available next week. Their answers may change the fields and confirmation process required in the product.
