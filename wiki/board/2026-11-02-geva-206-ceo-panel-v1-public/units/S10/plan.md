# StayTrellis Flex additions

## Why this release
StayTrellis books furnished stays for employers moving staff between offices. This release adds Flex, a monthly package of flexible departure dates and one mid-stay apartment change. The commercial goal is six additional employer contracts in the next two quarters, with at least 95% of booked employee nights fulfilled without an emergency move.

We will keep the current employer booking service unchanged. Flex uses a separately reserved pool of apartments and costs $85 per resident each month. Participating property operators have signed rates for the extra flexibility. The first release covers one city, where the supply team can serve up to 100 packages without reducing the contracted employer pool.

## Customers and purchase
Flex will be sold directly to arriving residents as a personal subscription. The arrival email will invite them to pay with their own card and select the dates and moves they want. Residents will be the buying audience for the offer, and the package will not be sold to employer mobility teams or included in employer procurement discussions. Our growth case is that personal subscriptions from residents will lead to the six new employer contracts.

Residents can cancel at the end of any month. A clear confirmation shows which apartment changes and departure dates their package covers. We will not offer unrelated travel discounts, loyalty points, or open-ended concierge work. Support uses the existing resident desk, with a dedicated operating allowance for apartment changes.

## Delivery work
Two engineers and one operations lead will deliver the package ledger, charge collection, supplier reservation view, and cancellation process in six weeks. The package ledger sits beside existing bookings so a package cancellation cannot cancel an employer-paid stay. Support will rehearse moves with the participating operators before invitations start.

The first wave consists of 100 eligible arrivals. They receive a free first month with the regular monthly price displayed before enrollment. No payment is taken without a separate confirmation. The operations team will reconcile the reserved supply every morning and stop new sales if spare capacity falls below the signed coverage level.

## Rollout rule
We will expand invitations to all four current cities when 60 residents activate the first-month package. That activation threshold alone authorizes the additional city spend. The decision will not wait for the first cohort's departure outcomes or the employer contract review. Each city's initial exposure remains capped at its signed apartment pool, with the same cancellation rights and staffing ratio.

## Reporting and ownership
The weekly report will show activations, paid continuation, net contribution, fulfilled nights, emergency moves, employer introductions, and signed employer contracts. The commercial director owns the six-contract goal. The supply lead owns fulfillment and can suspend individual buildings for poor service. Finance has approved $48,000 for construction and $24,000 for the initial city launch, with a further $60,000 allocated only when the rollout rule is met.
