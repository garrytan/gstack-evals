# Operating and launch evidence

A previous optional arrival service reached 74 activations from 100 free invitations. Only five continued at the stated price. Its first-month use increased emergency apartment changes because residents selected dates that the employer later reversed. Activation counts did not predict fulfilled nights or employer renewals. The trial records contain both activation and later service outcomes by resident.

The approved Flex pilot brief requires at least 95% fulfilled nights without emergency moves, positive contribution at the normal price, and two mobility-team purchase discussions before spending on another city. The brief allocates enough staff for 100 packages: the desk has 45 spare hours weekly against an estimated 31 hours of package work. Additional-city staffing is budgeted at the same ratio. Supplier prices leave $28 contribution per paid package after expected support and change costs.
