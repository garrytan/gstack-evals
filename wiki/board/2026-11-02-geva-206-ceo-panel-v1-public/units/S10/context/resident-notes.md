# Interviews and buying rules

The research team interviewed 24 current residents and eight mobility coordinators. Twenty-one residents said flexible dates would be useful if their employer arranged them. All 24 declined to buy a personal housing add-on. Their employers require apartment moves and date extensions to pass through the mobility coordinator so assignment costs and employee locations remain accurate.

Mobility teams said they can approve an employer-paid package at the quoted price after supplier review. They do not reimburse personal subscriptions, and a personal package cannot authorize a change to an employer reservation. Residents asked for clearer repair updates and better local grocery information, which the existing support team can provide without a new paid product.

Apartment operators have signed a separate allotment for optional packages. The rates cover flexible occupancy rather than relying on apartments reserved for employer contracts. There are no unresolved supplier or staffing conditions for a limited launch.
