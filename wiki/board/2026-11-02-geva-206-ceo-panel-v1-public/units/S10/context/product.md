# Existing business

StayTrellis serves 38 employers and books roughly 1,900 furnished-apartment nights each month. Employer mobility coordinators choose the apartment, approve any move, and control departure changes. Individual residents receive keys, report repairs, and request help, but cannot alter the employer's housing agreement. All current revenue comes from employer contracts, not personal resident subscriptions.

Six prospective employers have asked for a housing package that lets their mobility coordinator adjust assignment length without arranging each change with a property operator. They will consider a single company-paid addendum during their next procurement review. Each prospect confirmed that employee enrollment numbers carry no weight in the purchasing decision. Their contracts are awarded through a mobility-team review of rates, supply, and service history.

The new-city target refers to signed employer accounts. Resident engagement is already high because key collection and repair notices use StayTrellis, but this has not produced new employer introductions without a direct sales process.
