# Current service

TrayBrook coordinates prepared-meal orders between care homes and three local kitchens. Homes enter the next day's counts before 16:00. Kitchens receive a confirmed quantity and deliver the following morning. Menu substitutions are handled separately from quantities.

The northern route serves twelve homes. Six have volunteered for an ordering trial, and all six have an authorized purchaser and reliable weekday access to the existing web service. Kitchens have agreed to honor any commercially chosen order schedule at the current unit price.

The company does not currently provide meal badges or a staff recipe library. Those would be new capabilities. A home can continue its subscription without either. Existing kitchen and delivery capacity is sufficient for all planned trial orders.
