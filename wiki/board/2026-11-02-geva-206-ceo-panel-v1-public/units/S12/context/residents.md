# Occupancy and meal counts

The six trial homes recorded their daily occupancy and meal counts for eight weeks. Every home had occupancy changes after Monday in all eight weeks. The average absolute change between Monday and Friday was 14 percent of residents. Hospital stays, new admissions and short family visits account for the changes.

Meal quantities follow who is present on the day. A resident away overnight receives meals elsewhere, and an arriving resident needs food immediately. The homes cannot move these events to the beginning of a week. None can predict the whole week's count on Monday.

The kitchens already keep ingredients in flexible batches. Their records show that tomorrow's quantity can be adjusted without extra food cost up to the existing daily deadline. Delivered leftovers cannot be resold or held for another day's residents.
