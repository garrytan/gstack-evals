# Calls with meal coordinators

Meal coordinators want fewer unused delivered portions. They are satisfied with the current menus and unit prices. Five homes find daily quantity entry quick; the sixth has a deputy cover it on days off. All six can supply delivered and served meal counts for a trial.

The coordinators suggested easier count corrections and a weekly waste report. Each said they would try a focused ordering change without other new services. Their printed food-program descriptions were renewed this year. Care staff use their own recipes for occasional cooking activities and did not request a shared collection.

The customer lead has not offered badges or a recipe collection in any renewal negotiation. None of the six homes links its ordering decision to those additions. The homes are willing to start the ordering trial as soon as that part is available.
