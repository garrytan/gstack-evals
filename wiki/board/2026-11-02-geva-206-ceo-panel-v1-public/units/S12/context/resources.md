# Estimates and measurement

An order-schedule option and kitchen quantity report require five engineer-weeks. An additional seven weeks covers badge authoring, recipe collection and editorial preparation. A twelve-week release fits the assigned staff and the approved $72,000 budget, including the trial food reserve.

The route manager has time for six introductions and weekly follow-up. Each home already records occupied-resident days, served meals and top-up purchases for its own accounting. The current reporting service can receive aggregate counts without resident records.

Finance will compare waste alongside missed meals and top-up spending. There is no contract requiring weekly counts or a particular release date. Kitchens can continue daily orders indefinitely at current prices, and homes can leave the proposed option without a fee.
