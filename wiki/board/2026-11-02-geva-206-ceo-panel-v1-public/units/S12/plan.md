# TrayBrook weekly ordering

## Change requested

TrayBrook currently lets residential care homes order prepared meals for the next day. We will add a weekly-order option for the homes on our northern delivery route. The aim is to reduce meals that kitchens prepare but homes do not serve, while keeping the current meal price and delivery schedule.

The weekly option will set each home's breakfast, lunch and dinner quantities on Monday morning. Those quantities will remain fixed through Sunday. Kitchens will receive the full week's order immediately and prepare the agreed quantities each day. Homes can change the following week's order at the next Monday opening. Daily menu substitutions will still be allowed within the fixed quantity.

## Release contents

The weekly option will launch together with a new meal-badge authoring service and a staff recipe library. The badge service will let homes write short descriptions of their food program. The library will let care staff collect ideas for meals they prepare outside our deliveries. Both will be available to every home taking the weekly option.

We will complete all three pieces before the northern-route trial opens. The product team will use a common account and billing arrangement, with no new fee during the eight-week trial. After the trial, weekly ordering will be included in the home's existing subscription.

## Team and calendar

One engineer will spend three weeks on order schedules and two on kitchen reporting. A second engineer and the editor will spend another seven weeks on badges and the library. The coordinated release will open at the end of week twelve. These people are assigned and the $72,000 budget covers the full period.

The route manager will introduce the option in normal monthly calls. Participating homes already have purchasing approval and a named meal coordinator. Support will remain within the current weekday service hours. Kitchen preparation capacity and vehicle routes will not change.

## Checking the result

We will compare unserved meals per occupied-resident day during the trial with the previous eight weeks. Homes will record both delivered and served quantities. We will also track additional food purchases and missed meals, so a reduction in recorded waste cannot come from simply serving less food.

The trial will cover six homes. The remaining route will keep daily ordering until the trial shows a sustained reduction in waste without more missed meals or extra food purchases. The route manager owns that decision with the customer lead.

## Commercial terms

No home must take the weekly option. Each can return to daily ordering at the end of the trial. Kitchens will be paid for the quantities sent on their normal invoices, and the trial has a $5,000 food-cost reserve. The badge and recipe services contain ordinary food-program material, not care instructions or medical advice.
