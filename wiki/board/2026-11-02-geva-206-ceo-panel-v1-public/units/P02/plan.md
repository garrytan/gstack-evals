# Sorrelreach pickup assurance

## Service change
Sorrelreach provides donation-pickup scheduling for nonprofit furniture banks. We will add appointment assurance messages to reduce failed collections from 23 percent to below eight percent. The first release covers booked pickups in the three districts where the furniture banks already operate. We will include it in the banks' existing service fee rather than create a new donor subscription.

## Confirmation sequence
Donors will receive a message two days before collection and again two hours before the arrival window. Each message will show the agreed item list and ask the donor to confirm that it is ready. A donor can correct contact details or request a later date. A dispatcher will see the responses in the normal route view. The sequence will use the current appointment and item records, with no new household data collection.

The launch will retain current vehicle assignments, loading rules and route sizes. A donor's confirmation will mark the collection as ready for the driver. Drivers will keep their existing arrival windows and dispatch contact. We will not add transport capacity or alter which items fit a vehicle as part of this release. The messages will use plain language, and donors who prefer a telephone call will receive the same sequence from the existing dispatch desk.

## Messaging agreement
At project approval, we will pay $210,000 in advance for a five-year exclusive appointment-messaging agreement with Asterfold Relay. The payment is nonrefundable, there is no early termination or assignment right, and all Sorrelreach appointment messages must use that service for the full term. The product team will implement its standard templates during the build. The approved project reserve covers the payment, integration and the bounded release.

## Build and first group
Two engineers will add confirmations, response status and dispatcher visibility in five weeks. The operations lead will test message timing, opt-outs and the call alternative in week six. We will release to 150 booked pickups over the following four weeks, without increasing route volume. Dispatchers will continue to contact donors when the recorded item list changes.

## What we will measure
The primary measure is a completed collection, confirmed by the furniture bank's intake record. We will compare the first group with concurrent routes using the current process. We will also count rescheduling, donor complaints and dispatch time. A larger release requires fewer than eight percent failed collections, no increase in complaints and lower total cost per successful pickup. If those conditions are not met, we will stop adding routes and examine the recorded reasons before changing the service.

## Operating boundaries
No bank has to change its donor agreements, and donor phone numbers will remain controlled by the bank. Existing exports and dispatch access will stay available. We will not add new districts, item categories or a public furniture marketplace during the first release. Banks keep control of scheduling decisions and can decline a pickup under their normal acceptance rules.
