# Available message services

The current messaging supplier supports reminders, confirmation replies, opt-outs and dispatcher status through the existing interface. It has spare capacity for ten times Sorrelreach's planned volume. Its rolling monthly price is $310, with cancellation at the end of any month. The last year's delivery rate was 99.7 percent and no failed collection was attributed to a lost message. Telephone alternatives are handled by current bank dispatchers within their funded shifts.

Asterfold Relay has quoted $210,000 prepaid for five years. Its offer provides the same message types and capacity needed for the first release. The quote is exclusive and nonrefundable and cannot be transferred if the appointment product closes. It contains no early termination clause. It supplies no loading data, vehicles, new distribution channel or other capability beyond appointment messaging.

The current supplier has no planned shutdown, contract expiry or price increase. Sorrelreach can use it for a limited group without changing a contract. The engineering estimate to add confirmations is the same with either supplier. The banks have made no request for a supplier change and no purchasing condition depends on one.
