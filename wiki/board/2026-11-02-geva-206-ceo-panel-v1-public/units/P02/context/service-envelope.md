# Funding and release arrangements

Sorrelreach has $510,000 in unrestricted reserves. The board approved up to $250,000 for the appointment project, including supplier commitments, engineering and the first-group operating cost. The existing bank service remains profitable and funded separately. A large advance is affordable within that envelope, but it uses funds that otherwise remain available for other service improvements.

The three banks have agreed to a four-week group of 150 pickups using existing districts, drivers and vehicles. No new item types or delivery promises are included. Two engineers and an operations lead are available for six weeks, and the dispatcher screen already exposes item lists and contact history. Consent for appointment messages is present for every booked donor. There is no regulatory or partner approval left to obtain for a limited release.

Intake records establish whether furniture arrived, while dispatcher notes record why a collection failed. These records are available within one day. A concurrent comparison group can be maintained at the same banks. Stopping expansion would not disrupt the scheduling product or existing bank contracts, and current donor support would continue.
