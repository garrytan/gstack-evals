# Failed pickup review

Sorrelreach supports three nonprofit furniture banks. Of 300 recently booked collections, 69 failed. Dispatchers reviewed all 69 with the donor and driver. Fifty-eight failed because the assigned vehicle had no remaining loading space when it reached the donor. In each of those cases the donor was home and the stated items were ready. Seven failures involved illness or a changed donor schedule, and four involved an incorrect item description.

Current dispatchers already call every donor the day before collection and confirm readiness. Their call records and driver notes agree in 65 of the 69 failed cases; the remaining four were the item-description cases. A two-week test added a second call immediately before departure. Failed collections remained at 22 percent, and the donors reached by both calls were just as likely to face a full vehicle.

Vehicle plans count stops rather than the measured volume of furniture. An operations test kept the same fleet but used item-volume estimates to limit each route. In 74 collections it achieved a six percent failure rate. The furniture banks can supply item dimensions before scheduling. They do not need donor messaging to change their loading plan.
