# Kestrelwick accepted-to-start repair

## Result we need
Kestrelwick places temporary facilities workers at training campuses. Only 61 percent of accepted assignments currently reach a completed first shift. We will raise that figure to 80 percent by shortening the gap between an application and an accepted assignment. The first release will cover workers moving to a campus they have not served before.

## Direct offers
Applicants with a Ready badge will receive a confirmed assignment as soon as they choose an open shift. These assignments will go directly to the arrival schedule without an induction-booking step. The confirmation will include pay, arrival instructions, the campus contact and a cancellation link. Workers without a badge will use the current induction-booking flow.

We will retain the worker's explicit acceptance and will not enroll anyone automatically. A worker can hold only one assignment in an overlapping time period. The matching rules for travel distance, role and pay stay unchanged. Employers will approve the same pay rates they use today and receive the same attendance report.

## Four-week delivery
The team will add direct offers, shift locking and confirmation messages in weeks one and two. In week three, operations will rehearse the process with the six participating campuses. Week four will open it to a capped group of 120 Ready workers. Two engineers and a half-time operations lead have a fixed $52,000 budget. The existing offer and scheduling services support the volume, and the campus managers have agreed to the trial dates.

## Recording results
We will record time to acceptance, completed first shifts, worker complaints and paid shift revenue. The reporting team will preserve a comparable group on the current process. A completed shift requires employer attendance confirmation, not merely a worker check-in. The financial report will include support time and any paid cancellation compensation.

## Moving beyond the first group
After four weeks, we will activate direct offers for every Ready worker if at least 75 percent of offers are accepted within twelve hours. That acceptance threshold is the release decision. Completed-shift and complaint results will remain on the monthly dashboard for ongoing service management. The wider release has capacity for 1,400 assignments per month and uses existing account managers rather than a new sales campaign.

## What stays unchanged
There are no changes to pricing, employment arrangements or worker credential documents. Campuses keep their existing contact desk. Workers can export their assignment records. The team will not add new campus types or geographic areas this quarter. The current offer workflow will remain available for non-Ready applicants, and engineering will retain a switch to disable direct offers if the service becomes unavailable.
