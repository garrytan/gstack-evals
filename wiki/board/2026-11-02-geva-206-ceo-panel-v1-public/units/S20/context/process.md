# Campus induction and badge meanings

The Ready badge records completion of general employment checks and one campus induction. It remains on the worker profile when that worker applies elsewhere. Every campus requires its own on-site induction before a worker's first paid shift there. The badge does not carry that local induction to another campus. The site requirement is part of each participating employer's operating agreement and cannot be waived by Kestrelwick.

All 120 workers selected for the proposed trial hold a Ready badge from a different campus. None has completed induction at the destination campus. Destination desks will not admit a worker to the shift until that appointment is complete. A current induction slot can be reserved along with an offer; six campuses have made slots available throughout the trial window.

The badge label was shortened two years ago, but the underlying meaning did not change. The current scheduling team checks the campus induction record as well as the badge. The existing offer service can call the same record and reserve a slot. Campus contacts and the required induction locations are already present in the employer directory.
