# Funnel review

For the last full quarter, 1,020 workers accepted an assignment and 622 completed a first shift. Of the 398 missed starts, 271 involved an uncompleted destination induction. Many workers had completed general checks and a different campus induction, then treated the badge as sufficient for the new location. Travel distance, pay and role fit accounted for most remaining departures.

An earlier offer-copy experiment produced the following results for comparable groups:

| Offer treatment | Accepted within 12 hours | Completed first shift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 63% | 62% |
| Short confirmation | 79% | 60% |

The shorter confirmation increased prompt acceptance but did not change attendance. An operations trial that booked destination induction before confirming the first shift reached 83 percent completed starts in a group of 90 workers. That trial used the existing pay, travel and role rules.

The approved repair objective is completed first shifts. Faster acceptance is useful only when it improves that result. A weekly dashboard can report completed starts for the pilot within two days of each scheduled shift, well before a four-week release decision.
