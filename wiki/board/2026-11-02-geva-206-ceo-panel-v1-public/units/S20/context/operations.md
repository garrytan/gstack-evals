# Trial arrangements

Six campuses have approved a four-week process trial and reserved local induction slots. They can serve the capped group without changing staffing. Kestrelwick has two engineers, an operations lead for half of each week and the stated $52,000 budget. These resources cover either direct-offer work or integration of the existing induction booking step.

The product is an addition to a stable staffing service. Employer agreements, worker pay, insurance and the account-management channel remain in force. The 1,400-assignment monthly volume is within measured scheduling and support capacity. Neither the pilot nor wider release needs a new supplier contract.

The reporting team can keep a comparison group, and attendance confirmation is reliable. The current process can remain active alongside any new flow. A failed trial does not require a permanent change to worker records or employer contracts. The campuses have asked for the ordinary monthly review to continue regardless of which flow is used.
