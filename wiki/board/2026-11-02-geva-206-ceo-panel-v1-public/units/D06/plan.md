# Fernettle leave desk repair

## Service result
Fernettle administers leave requests for employers with rotating shifts. Requests arriving outside office hours now wait until the next working day. We will give employees a human response within two hours at any time, so they can complete their request before their next shift. The target is to reduce leave requests abandoned before submission from 29 percent to below 10 percent.

## A continuous response desk
The promise will apply twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week to the 36 participating employers. Our existing weekday team will handle office hours. The contracted night-and-weekend desk will cover all remaining hours, public holidays and overflow. Both teams will use the same approved leave instructions and will record the next action in the existing case system.

A response means a trained adviser has reviewed the request and either helped the employee complete it or explained exactly what is missing. It does not mean that leave is approved. Employer managers retain the approval decision and their existing response obligations. Advisers cannot change eligibility rules or promise a paid entitlement.

## Product changes
We will add case assignment across the two desks, a visible response deadline and a handover record. The employee can reply through the current secure messaging channel. We will not add a new application, employee charge or separate employer buying process. The employer roster, language preferences and consent controls remain in the current product.

Two engineers will deliver the routing and handover work in four weeks. Week five will run a staffed rehearsal using synthetic requests followed by consenting employer volunteers. The release will begin with twelve employers for two weeks and then reach all 36 if service tests pass. No other employer will enter the guarantee until capacity is contracted.

## Funding and oversight
The annual service budget is $264,000, covered by signed employer addenda. It includes the outside desk's fixed charge, current staff allocation, software costs and a contingency reserve. An operations manager will audit a sample of completed requests each week. The desk provider must supply named trained advisers and report any missed response deadline on the same day.

## Gates for release and continuation
The first twelve employers must achieve at least 98 percent of human responses within two hours, fewer than two percent incorrect instructions and a reduction in abandoned submissions. The full service must keep those thresholds over the next eight weeks. We will pause new enrollment if any threshold is missed and use the provider's contracted reserve for existing customers. Employees will always see whether a request is awaiting information or employer approval. Renewal of the desk contract will depend on completed submissions, adviser accuracy and net service contribution, not contact volume alone.
