# Signed desk agreement

Fernettle has signed a twelve-month agreement with Mallowcrest Response Cooperative. The agreement starts two weeks before release rehearsal. It covers every hour outside Fernettle's weekday office schedule, including public holidays. It also supplies overflow during office hours. The cooperative must maintain two concurrent advisers and an additional reserve adviser who joins within fifteen minutes of an alert.

The fixed annual charge is $132,000. The agreement includes employer-specific training, secure case-system access, quality audits and a two-hour human-response service level. Named advisers have completed training and signed confidentiality terms. A replacement desk arrangement covers a provider outage, with its retainer included in the quoted charge. The cooperative cannot redirect these reserved advisers to another customer during the covered hours.

Peak observations were seven new requests an hour, with fourteen adviser minutes per request. Two advisers provide 120 adviser minutes an hour against 98 minutes of work at that peak; reserve capacity covers measured bursts and absence. The capacity test used ten requests an hour for four hours and met the two-hour response target. Existing Fernettle staff have the same two-adviser office-hours coverage.
