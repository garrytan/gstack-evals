# Budget and delivery readiness

All 36 employers have signed addenda totaling $264,000 annually for the continuous desk. Costs are $132,000 for the cooperative, $86,000 for the existing staff allocation, $18,000 for software and auditing, and $28,000 held as contingency. The first-year engineering budget of $42,000 is separately approved. No future hire or expected customer sale is included as available capacity or funding.

Employers have approved the advice scripts and will keep their own approval rules. The provider has completed access review and can work inside the current case system. The system records who advised an employee and preserves the distinction between help completing a request and an employer decision. Employees already have accounts and messages reach the same application they use for leave today.

The two-week first group has similar shift patterns to the full population. Employer addenda permit a pause in adding accounts if the service gate fails; guarantees already activated remain covered by the signed desks. The annual provider agreement has a service-failure termination clause and transition support. No historical leave data will be removed during the repair.
