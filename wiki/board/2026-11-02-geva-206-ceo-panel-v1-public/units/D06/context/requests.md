# Shift-worker leave study

Fernettle serves 36 employers whose employees frequently begin shifts before the normal helpdesk opens. In the last quarter, 29 percent of started leave requests were abandoned before submission. In 81 reviewed cases, 62 employees stopped because a simple document or eligibility question went unanswered until after their shift began. Employer approval delay accounted for seven cases, and unrelated reasons accounted for the remaining twelve.

A six-week evening trial put a trained adviser on the same messaging channel. At the trial sites, abandonment fell to nine percent, with no change in employer approval time. Employees could finish the submission when the adviser explained which evidence was needed. Trial advice accuracy was 99 percent under the employer-approved instructions.

Employers asked for the service to cover all shift patterns and agreed to fund it through their existing contracts. The target is request completion, not more leave approvals. The current application already supports asynchronous secure messages, document uploads and employer decisions. No employee is required to contact the desk if they can complete a request without assistance.
