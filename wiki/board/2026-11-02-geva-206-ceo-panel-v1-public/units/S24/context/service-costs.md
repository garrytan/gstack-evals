# Measured monthly effort

The twelve-charity trial used the same small-charity eligibility limits as the proposed launch. Each account required at least two analyst hours per monthly cycle, including import review, customer questions and approval preparation. The observed average was 2.3 hours. No account fell below two hours across the three cycles. Analyst contracts charge $28 per productive hour, including employment overhead.

Secure report delivery and account-specific processing cost another $6 monthly per charity. These costs continue at eighty and at eight hundred accounts. The operations estimate assumes reuse of the software queue and bulk bank import; neither removes the individual charity investigation and approval work. There is no contracted automation or lower-cost labor source that changes the two-hour minimum.

The core software subscription covers its existing hosting, support and development obligations at the approved margin. The board has not allocated that margin or a grant to support the reconciliation service. The first-quarter cash reserve finances working capital only and must be replenished from service receipts. Eighty accounts need at least 160 productive analyst hours monthly; four contracted analysts have 200 hours available, so the planned account limit fits their delivery capacity.
