# Account loss study

Alderwake serves 420 local community charities. In the last year, 24 percent canceled. Forty-three departing customers named unresolved donor matching as their main reason. They receive bank deposits with abbreviated references, and volunteer treasurers often cannot work out which household made a payment. Export and reporting functions otherwise score well in account reviews.

A paid manual trial served twelve charities for three monthly cycles. Analysts resolved 94 percent of uncertain matches, and participating treasurers saved a median of five hours a month. All twelve wanted to continue. Nine signed letters accepting up to $95 per month for this service. The other three needed committee approval. The trial supports a managed reconciliation offer for the selected segment rather than a wider accounting service.

Customers already use Alderwake's monthly bank import and have authority to share these records with its trained analysts under their current agreements. No new bank connection is needed. The company has not promised to keep the old reconciliation archive indefinitely, and retaining or removing it is a product decision rather than a regulatory requirement.
