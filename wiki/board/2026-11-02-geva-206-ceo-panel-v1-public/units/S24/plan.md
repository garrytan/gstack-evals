# Alderwake reconciliation service

## Retention repair
Alderwake provides donor administration software to local community charities. We will add a managed monthly reconciliation service to reduce cancellations caused by unresolved donor identities and unmatched deposits. The service will turn a bank deposit list and donation records into a reviewed set of donor matches, exceptions and corrections. The goal is to reduce annual cancellation among participating charities from 24 percent to below 12 percent.

## What customers receive
Each subscriber will have one named reconciliation analyst. The analyst will review the monthly bank import, resolve uncertain matches with the charity and return a signed completion report. Customers will approve corrections before they enter the donor ledger. The service includes all of a charity's monthly deposits, with the first group restricted to the small-charity segment described in the operating brief.

The product team will build an analyst queue, an approval record and a monthly report within the current application. We will keep the donor ledger, payment collection and exports unchanged. Customers will receive a clear list of any unresolved items rather than estimated matches. The customer success team has direct access to every eligible buyer and will offer enrollment during their regular account review.

## Price and continuing operation
The service will cost $24 per charity per month, added to its existing subscription. This will be the continuing price for the launch segment, not an introductory offer. There will be no usage allowance, reconciliation fee or later price adjustment. The service will support itself from that charge; core software subscription revenue remains allocated to the existing product. We will recruit up to eighty charities initially and continue the same offer as the service grows.

## Historical data at changeover
On the day each charity joins the new workflow, we will permanently delete its former reconciliation archive, including historical alias decisions and analyst notes. The new queue will begin with current donor names, open deposits and the active alias list. We will remove the older archive from live storage and backups as one completed migration task. There will be no parallel archive or historical archive export. All new monthly reports will be exportable.

## Launch and control
Three engineers have eight weeks and $88,000 to build the queue and approval workflow. Four trained analysts are funded from service revenue, with cash reserved for the first operating quarter. They can cover eighty monthly accounts. The first ten charities will complete two monthly cycles before the next seventy join.

We will track matched deposits, correction reversals, customer hours saved, cancellation and net service contribution. Expansion beyond eighty requires at least a twelve-point reduction in cancellations, positive net contribution and no increase in ledger corrections. Enrollment can pause between groups without affecting the underlying software. Charity officers retain final control over every correction.
