# Operating baseline
Across the eighteen observed clinics, the median was six overdraft days and $430 in bank charges per quarter. Revenue was stable within 4%. Cash forecasting error averaged 7% for scheduled outflows and 18% for receipts. All clinics have read-only bank exports and invoice files that can be shared with an approved processor.

There is no requirement to initiate payments in a first release. Clinics already use their accounting system for the ledger and want to retain it. Data can be deleted at cancellation after the customer downloads its reports.

The test budget includes $8,000 for customer recruitment and $12,000 for the operations specialist. No launch expenditure depends on outside financing. The founders can extend testing by four weeks without adding staff or signing another annual contract.
