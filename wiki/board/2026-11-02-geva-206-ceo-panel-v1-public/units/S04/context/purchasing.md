# Buying arrangements
All forty clinics on the intended launch list are members of the Alderbrook Clinic Buying Circle. Membership covers clinical purchasing and third-party financial software. Its agreements prohibit members from subscribing to financial software outside the circle's approved catalog, including subscriptions paid with a manager's personal card and later reimbursed.

The circle has an intake route for new financial tools. A vendor submits a standard service agreement, a data-handling summary and two clinic references. Review takes three weeks. Approved vendors can run circle-sponsored trials and use a managed signup link. The circle takes no sales commission.

The newsletter accepts advertisements from any vendor. Its publisher does not perform approval or forward leads to the circle. Managers read it for professional news, but cannot use an advertisement as purchasing authorization.
