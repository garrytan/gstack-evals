# Company and objective
Cedarwake is a new cash-planning company founded by a clinic bookkeeper and two software builders. It has no lending license and will not hold customer funds. The board approved a $96,000 build and trial budget. Current staffing covers a ten-week build and four months of support for forty clinics.

The intended customers are independent animal clinics with one to four locations. Their finance managers already prepare weekly cash forecasts, but the work takes two to three hours. The commercial objective is fewer overdraft days at the same credit limit, not a reduction in bookkeeping headcount. Clinics surveyed would pay $90 to $140 monthly for a demonstrated reduction in bank charges.

The founders have five clinics available for supervised product testing. Participation in those tests does not include permission to purchase an ongoing subscription.
