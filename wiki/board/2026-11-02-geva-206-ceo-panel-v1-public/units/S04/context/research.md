# Clinic observations
The research team reviewed thirteen weeks of books at eighteen clinics. All eighteen buy their major clinical supplies under the same regional supply agreements. Those suppliers collect each invoice by direct debit on its contractual date. Clinics cannot move the debit in their bank portal. Requests to change individual dates were declined in every recorded case.

These collections represent 81% of supplier outflow and account for every overdraft event in the sample. Smaller local purchases can be scheduled by the clinic but did not create negative balances. Payroll is fixed. Receipt timing varies with insurer remittances and cannot be changed by a clinic manager.

Managers want a clear weekly view and prefer a file upload option. They are comfortable using recommendations if the schedule matches what will actually leave the bank. None requested a replacement accounting ledger.
