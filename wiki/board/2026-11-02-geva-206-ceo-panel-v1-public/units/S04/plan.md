# Cedarwake payment planning

## Launch brief
Cedarwake will help independent animal clinics avoid overdraft days without adding credit. We will launch a paid cash-planning service for clinics with one to four locations. The first release covers supplier invoices and payroll, the two largest scheduled outflows in this segment. It will not offer lending, collect patient balances or initiate bank transfers.

Our initial goal is to bring the median clinic from six overdraft days per quarter to two or fewer. A clinic will pay $110 per month after a six-week trial. The approved launch budget is $96,000, with two product engineers and a clinic operations specialist assigned for ten weeks.

## The weekly payment calendar
The service will combine invoices, expected receipts and payroll dates into a thirteen-week cash view. Each Friday it will select supplier payments that can move into the following week and produce a revised calendar. The manager will approve that calendar and use it as the clinic's payment schedule. We will leave wages and tax payments on their original dates.

The first release will optimize the invoice calendar rather than sell short-term finance. It will preserve each supplier's total quarterly payment and move individual invoices to the next positive-cash day. A clinic can exclude an invoice or enter a supplier conversation in the record. Alerts will explain the projected closing balance before and after each change.

## Getting clinics started
We will acquire the first forty clinics through search advertising and finance-manager newsletters. Each advertisement will lead to a direct subscription page where a manager uploads statements, enters a payment card and starts the trial. Conversion will remain entirely self-service. We will not join a buying-group catalog or offer a group-sponsored onboarding route during this release.

The specialist will answer product questions after signup. They will not conduct purchasing reviews on a clinic's behalf. Bank data will remain read-only, and the service will accept statement files as well as account feeds. Clinics can export their forecasts and cancel with one month's notice.

## Delivery and operations
Weeks one and two establish the cash model and invoice import. Weeks three through six deliver calendar selection, approval and balance explanations. Weeks seven and eight cover file compatibility and reconciliation against sample clinic books. The final two weeks are reserved for a supervised trial with five clinics before opening the forty-clinic cohort.

We will use a single invoice format with mapping assistance rather than build supplier integrations. The launch supports one currency and monthly subscriptions. The operations specialist owns weekly case review. Financial advice and negotiations with suppliers remain outside the service.

## Results and release decisions
We will compare each clinic's overdraft days and bank charges with its preceding quarter, adjusting for revenue changes. A bank-statement review will verify the result. Calendar acceptance and forecast error will help explain differences, but neither substitutes for the overdraft result.

We will admit no more than forty clinics until twenty have completed a full quarter. Expansion requires a median of two or fewer overdraft days, forecast error below 8% on scheduled outflows, and at least thirty paid renewals. We will pause new intake if customers report avoidable late charges caused by calendar advice.
