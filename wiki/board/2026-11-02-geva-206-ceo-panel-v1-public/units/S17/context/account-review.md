# Current service and losses

VisitMoss supports bookings, listing pages, reminders, and attended or unattended viewing appointments. Property managers pay a flat monthly fee per building. The current recovery goal is completed first viewings and renewal of five existing managers. Those managers remain willing to renew if the wasted bookings stop. Prices and customer support response times are not cited as reasons to leave.

Among 400 recent bookings at the five firms, 148 did not become a viewing. In 119 cases the unit was already let before the appointment was made. Managers batch their status updates on Friday, leaving recently let units bookable for several days. The booking desk has no other source of availability. The other 29 cases were ordinary tenant cancellations, illness, or weather. The same mix appears across buildings with and without parking.

Each manager can supply an immediate status notification when it signs a lease. Two already do so for a separate advertising channel. A small change to the account process could use those notices to close VisitMoss bookings without replacing the booking system.
