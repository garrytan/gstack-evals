# Renter and manager conversations

Researchers called 31 renters whose appointments did not happen. Twenty-five said the manager told them the apartment had already gone. None reported abandoning a visit because of finding parking, reaching the building, or understanding the neighborhood. Twenty-eight had opened the existing street-map link. They rated finding the address as easy, including renters arriving by bus.

Managers interviewed about renewal identified repeated bookings for already let apartments as the issue that causes refund requests and extra phone work. They described neighborhood information as a nice addition to listings but did not connect it to the decision to renew. The current map and building address are accurate. The content specialist has time to produce forty guides, and managers can approve copy within two days.

The unattended service is attractive to the three managers with limited evening staff. Demand for those slots has been established in their existing accompanied-viewing calendars. It does not require a new renter registration flow or a new price.
