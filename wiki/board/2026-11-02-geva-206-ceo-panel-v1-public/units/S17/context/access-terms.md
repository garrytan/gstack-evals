# Building access process

Unattended viewings use keys released by a contracted building-access operator. Before releasing a key for any new building, the operator needs a signed owner authorization naming VisitMoss and the managing agent. A manager's general listing agreement is not enough. The operator normally takes five working days after receipt to activate a building. It cannot provide temporary keys or override an absent owner signature.

None of the twelve proposed buildings has this authorization. Owners have agreed to review the ordinary form, and no substantive negotiation is expected. The account lead can send it at the start of the project. Until activation, only the managers' accompanied appointments can enter those buildings. Those managers do not have staff assigned to accompany the newly proposed unattended slots. The existing access service works at 74 other buildings, has ample spare capacity, and charges a known fee already included in the recovery budget.
