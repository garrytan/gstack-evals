# VisitMoss viewing recovery

## Recovery target
VisitMoss helps small property managers arrange apartment viewings. Completed first viewings have fallen from 82% to 63% of bookings, and five management firms have paused renewal discussions. We will bring completion back above 80% and win renewal from four of those firms within eight weeks. We will work with the current manager accounts rather than seek a new renter segment.

The recovery team has one product engineer, one content specialist, and an account lead. We will use the existing booking and tenant contact systems. The budget is $28,000, including local research and account training. No price increase or annual lock-in is part of the recovery offer.

## Arrival confidence package
The central product change is an arrival guide for each building. It will show parking photographs, the easiest walking approach, nearby transport stops, a street map, and a two-minute neighborhood orientation. We will make arrival confidence the focus of the recovery work so renters keep their first viewing appointment.

The confirmation email will link to the guide immediately after booking. An optional text on the morning of the viewing will repeat the meeting location. Renters can download or print the guide without creating another account. Managers can approve building descriptions and correct transport details through their current account contact. We will retain the present listing-status update process and will not change how units become available or unavailable in this release.

## Assisted-viewing relaunch
Three current managers want to shift some viewings from accompanied visits to the existing unattended-viewing service. This is already a supported VisitMoss service at other buildings, and it will give these managers more viewing hours. We will add their twelve buildings to the recovery cohort and train the managers during week 2.

The booking calendar will open unattended slots in week 3, with the first visits occurring that Friday. In week 4 the account lead will send the building owners the standard access-service authorization. We will collect the signed forms in week 5 and then add them to the permanent account records. Existing accompanied appointments remain available outside the newly opened slots.

## Weekly delivery
Week 1 covers guide templates and selection of forty affected units. Weeks 2 and 3 cover building photography, manager approvals, and release of guides into booking confirmations. Week 4 extends the guides to all current listings in the cohort. We will freeze new content after that point and use weeks 5 through 8 for outcome review and corrections.

## Results and limits
The primary measure is completed first viewings divided by all bookings made during the recovery window. We will retain cancellations in the denominator and report unavailable-unit cancellations separately. We will compare each manager with its preceding eight weeks, record the renewal decision, and track renter complaints. A manager counts as recovered only after signing its ordinary renewal at the existing price. The account lead will call each paused customer in weeks 4 and 8. No additional city or new paid service will launch during this effort.
