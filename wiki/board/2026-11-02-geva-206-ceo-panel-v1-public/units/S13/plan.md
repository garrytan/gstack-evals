# Larchspan career exchange

## Product decision
Larchspan will add an exchange for employees seeking a different role. The current product helps employers publish internal vacancies and supports employee applications. The exchange will add external opportunities and paid recruiter participation. Our aim is to raise successful employee moves by twenty percent while retaining the employer contracts that fund the core product.

Employees will choose whether to activate a profile. They can state the role, location and working pattern they want, then accept or decline introductions. Recruiters pay $180 per completed introduction, with a refund if the profile is materially inaccurate. Existing employer pricing stays unchanged. Confirmed recruiter orders cover the initial service cost.

## Availability of profiles
An employee who activates a profile will appear in internal and external search immediately. External recruiters can request an introduction from the first day, and the employee can accept without a manager's approval. We will apply this availability rule to all employer accounts at launch. Internal teams will continue to see the same profiles and can contact an employee through the current application workflow.

Only the employee's selected information will be shared. We will not scrape records or reveal performance notes. An employee can close the profile at any time and decline any introduction. Recruiters must agree to named-contact rules and cannot download a bulk directory.

## Required launch package
The exchange will open only when it includes role matching, a salary research center, an employer-brand film studio, eighteen industry career guides and a coach booking service. These will be delivered as one release in month ten. The studio will offer recording appointments and editing for employer teams. Coaches will publish fixed-price appointments with clear cancellation terms. We will commission the career guides and maintain their annual updates within the approved budget.

The first matching workflow takes ten weeks. The research center, studio, guides and coaching service occupy the remaining schedule. No part of the exchange will be offered to customers before the full package is complete. Existing internal vacancies will continue operating during this period.

## Team and operating plan
Four engineers, a designer and a content lead have ten months of funding, totaling $960,000. A contracted studio and coaching administrator have reserved the launch capacity at fixed prices. We will reuse existing profile controls, employer sign-in and search infrastructure. The release does not include payroll changes, relocation services or employment advice.

## Evidence after release
We will count completed moves, time to an accepted introduction, employer renewal and employee opt-outs. We will expand recruiter access only if completed moves rise twenty percent and employer renewal remains at least 94 percent. A monthly employer council will review the aggregate results. Recruitment income will be reported net of refunds, studio costs and coaching administration rather than as gross booking value.
