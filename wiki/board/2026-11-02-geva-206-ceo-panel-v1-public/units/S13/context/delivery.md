# Estimates and controls

The engineering estimate for role matching, recruiter access and introduction tracking is ten weeks using the existing profile service. A separate salary center requires six weeks. The film studio setup and customer booking workflow require eight weeks. Career guides and coach booking add twelve weeks with content production in parallel. A combined launch therefore falls in month ten rather than month three.

The approved budget can cover the combined package. This is not a staffing or cash shortfall. Unspent funds may remain in reserve, and the board has authorized a smaller first release. Delaying matching by seven months postpones the paid introduction orders and the employee moves the team intends to improve. There is no shared procurement condition requiring all five services to start together.

Both recruiter introductions and the optional services have positive contribution after their direct costs. Existing internal vacancy support remains funded. Employer renewal data and completed-move data are available for all participating accounts, and the first group can be limited without changing the wider product.
