# Exchange research

The team interviewed 84 employees who had tried to move in the previous year. Fifty-six wanted a route to an external role if no suitable internal position appeared. Their most important requirements were matching by working pattern, control over disclosure and the ability to decline an introduction. A ten-week matching release met the concept-test acceptance conditions for 48 of those 56 employees.

Recruiters signed first-year orders for 500 introductions at $180 each. They need role fit, current availability and employee consent. They did not request career guides, a media studio, coaching or salary research. They will purchase matching by itself. Employers likewise agreed to trial a standalone matching addition within their existing profile timing rules.

Seven employees asked for salary articles, and three employer communications teams liked the idea of short films. Those requests were recorded for later consideration. No research participant made coaching, films or guides a condition of using matching. The content and media functions do not supply data needed for matching or consent.
