# Employer commitments

Larchspan serves 62 medium-sized employers. Employer subscriptions supply 92 percent of current revenue. The signed service schedule reserves the first 45 days of an employee's active mobility profile for internal opportunities. External introductions are permitted from day 46 if the employee consents. Employers accepted external opportunities on those terms because internal teams need a complete staffing cycle to offer a move.

The board ranked retention of these employer agreements above new recruiter revenue for the coming year. No employer has waived the 45-day interval. The standard renewal review explicitly checks adherence to the interval. In the most recent council, 51 employers confirmed that they would retain the service with external matching after the interval and would end the agreement if outside recruiters could approach employees during it.

Employees control participation and the contents of their profiles. The interval does not require manager permission to apply internally or externally after it ends. The company may develop external matching now without changing any agreement, provided it honors the existing timing. Recruiter purchase orders accept profiles becoming available on day 46.
