# Brackenbell learner recognition release

## Outcome for this quarter
Brackenbell manages small-group tutoring for independent learning centers. We will add learner recognition to increase next-term center renewals from 72 percent to 85 percent. The release will give center directors a visible record of the progress their tutors deliver and a useful item to share with families at term end. It will be included in the current center subscription.

## Recognition package
Tutors will select from six achievement descriptions and attach a short written observation. The product will create a printable certificate and a family-facing progress page. Directors can approve a batch before it is sent. Families can download the certificate without opening an account. A learner can receive recognition more than once, but each certificate records its date and the relevant learning group.

This quarter's product work will cover recognition only. Session coverage and replacement-tutor booking remain on the current service. We will use the existing family consent controls and learner roster. Tutors will not enter scores, public rankings or comparative labels. Directors will choose whether to send a certificate, and no automated message will go to a family without their approval.

## Center launch service
All 100 contracted centers will receive an individual three-hour launch clinic in the first five business days of the new term. A Brackenbell specialist will prepare a real class with the director, complete one certificate cycle and agree the center's family communication schedule. The clinics are part of the release commitment and will not be group sessions. The two current customer specialists will deliver them while keeping their normal helpdesk duties. Each clinic requires one specialist throughout; recordings will be available afterward for the center's staff.

## Build and release
Two engineers will implement templates and approvals in weeks one through four. The designer and five volunteer center directors will test plain-language descriptions, print layouts and permissions in week five. Week six is reserved for corrections and release preparation. The approved $74,000 budget covers this work, existing storage and launch materials. We will reuse the current messaging and document-generation services.

## Measurement and safeguards
The primary result is actual center renewal at the next term boundary. We will compare centers by prior renewal history and learner count, and record stated reasons for renewal or departure. Directors will also report family response and time spent producing recognition. A successful release improves renewal by at least ten points without adding more than twenty minutes of tutor administration per class per month.

We will stop further template work if the first term does not improve renewal. A director can disable recognition without changing tutoring schedules. Existing subscriptions, exports and helpdesk channels will remain in place throughout the release.
