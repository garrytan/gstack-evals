# Customer operations allocation

Two customer specialists are employed for the coming term. Each works forty hours per week, with ten hours reserved for breaks, internal meetings and leave coverage. The normal helpdesk requires twenty specialist hours per person per week. That leaves ten hours per person for planned customer appointments, or twenty appointment hours in the first five business days.

The operations manager has not approved overtime, temporary staff or helpdesk closure. The hiring freeze continues through the end of the term. Clinics cannot be delivered concurrently by the same person. Previous three-hour center clinics used the full three hours with a specialist, including live roster preparation and director training. Recordings reduced follow-up questions but did not shorten the booked clinic itself.

All 100 centers will begin the new term in the same week. Directors can take later appointments if those are offered in advance. Their contracts require access to the product and the existing helpdesk, but do not require individual training during the first week. A staggered appointment calendar or smaller first group would fit the current service agreement.
