# Why centers leave

Brackenbell has 100 contracted learning centers and manages their group schedules, tutor assignments and family communications. Last term, 28 of 100 eligible centers did not renew. Interviews covered 25 departures. Twenty-two named repeated uncovered sessions after tutor cancellations as the deciding factor. Directors reported that families left when a group lost two or more sessions in a month.

Certificates were mentioned in seven general feature interviews, mainly by centers that intended to renew anyway. In a forced-choice exercise, 31 of 34 directors chose reliable replacement coverage over richer progress materials. All 34 already had a printable certificate template and could send it through the current family messaging tool. None reported losing a family because a certificate was unavailable.

A manual replacement desk ran for four weeks at twelve centers. The same tutor pool filled 86 percent of otherwise uncovered sessions when staff were shown the open requests early. Those centers completed more paid sessions and all twelve renewed. This service has not yet been offered to other centers. The research team recommends measuring session completion and center renewal in future trials; certificate requests are also retained in the general feature log.
