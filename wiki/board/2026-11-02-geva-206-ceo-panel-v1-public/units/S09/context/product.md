# Product and funding notes

The current subscription covers scheduling, learner records, family consent and messaging. The proposed recognition feature adds reusable observations, approval of certificate batches and a retained family progress page. The existing printable template does not provide those functions. Five directors have volunteered to test them, and all have authority to turn them on at their own centers.

The engineering team has delivered a document-based feature of comparable size in six weeks. Its estimate includes permissions, deletion, accessible print output and approval testing. The $74,000 budget is approved and does not draw on the customer operations payroll. Recognition has low storage and message costs and creates no new paid service obligation after launch training.

Renewal outcomes are available from signed contracts, not surveys alone. The company can keep the release limited to current centers and can disable it without disturbing learner records. No external partner agreement, content license or new payment method is needed.
