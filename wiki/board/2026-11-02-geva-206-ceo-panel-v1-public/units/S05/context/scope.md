# Research priorities and delivery estimates

The practices' current session-change process uses email and a shared spreadsheet. In the past quarter, 31 sessions remained empty after a change because administrators could not match availability quickly. They estimate that a shared exchange register could reduce those gaps.

All three administrators said an exchange-only service would be useful and purchasable. A product estimate puts that service at four weeks using the company's current account infrastructure. The first useful trial does not depend on invoices, education or public listings.

Supplier invoices already travel through each practice's finance process. Course purchases have separate annual budgets and occur a few times a year. None of the practice owners made those capabilities a condition of adopting session exchanges. They did not request a public professional directory.
