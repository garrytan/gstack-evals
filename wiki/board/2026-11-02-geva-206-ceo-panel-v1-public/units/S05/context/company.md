# Business objective

FennelBridge sells administrative tools to outpatient providers. SessionWillow is a proposed new line, separate from its billing product. The six-month target is ten paying practice groups and a measurable increase in filled consultation sessions after staff schedule changes.

Finance has approved $160,000 and reserved two engineers for twenty weeks. A coordinator can support the expected account count without additional hiring. The company can afford the complete proposed schedule; no other release depends on these people.

Practice groups can purchase subscriptions through the company's existing invoicing route. There is no public procurement process. The three research practices have requested a small group-level session-exchange service. Each is willing to begin a paid trial once its scheduling administrator can control allocations. No existing company product supports session exchanges.
