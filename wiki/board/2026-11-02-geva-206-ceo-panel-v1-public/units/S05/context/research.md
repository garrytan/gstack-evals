# Roles and purchasing

We observed scheduling at three outpatient groups and interviewed nine dietitians and six practice administrators. Dietitians send availability to administrators, but do not allocate rooms, move funded clinic sessions or authorize substitutes. All allocation changes must be accepted by a practice administrator.

The practices prohibit staff from connecting personal paid scheduling tools to the practice schedule. They do not reimburse personal administration subscriptions. The interviewed dietitians said they would not pay for a tool they could not use to confirm an exchange.

Administrators each control a software budget. They offered to pay $120 per practice per month for a group account that lets them approve exchanges and see the weekly allocation. Dietitians would contribute availability inside that account. Both roles can access a web service from their usual work devices.
