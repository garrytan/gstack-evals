# Supplier and service notes

Two course providers have signed nonexclusive catalog agreements with no minimum sales or prepaid inventory. They can fulfill course purchases through their existing services. Displaying their catalog is optional and can begin at any time during the year.

The proposed directory records course attendance only. It would not contain patient comments or clinical ratings. The company already has appropriate rights to the professional profiles supplied for research, although publication remains optional for each contributor.

The approved staffing calendar supports the twenty-week release and six months of weekday support. Practice-level reporting can count filled sessions without patient details. The owners have agreed to provide baseline and follow-up coverage numbers for a trial limited to their own sites.
