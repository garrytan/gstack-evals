# SessionWillow product proposal

## Purpose and customer

SessionWillow is a new paid scheduling companion for dietitians working in multi-site outpatient practices. It will help practices keep more consultation time available when a practitioner changes shifts. Our business target is ten paying practices within six months of release.

We will put the individual dietitian at the center of the offer. Each practitioner will buy a $19 monthly account, select the sites they work at and publish requests to exchange sessions. The account holder will accept another dietitian's request and confirm the revised allocation. Sales will focus on individual practitioners rather than practice administrators. A personal account will remain the unit of purchase.

## What ships together

The release will include session exchanges, a supplier-invoice workspace, a continuing-education course marketplace and a public practitioner rating directory. All four will open on the same date. We will finish the complete offer before inviting paid users, so each new account has a broad home for professional administration.

The exchange service will support recurring sessions at several sites and a weekly record of accepted exchanges. The invoice workspace will cover equipment and educational expenses. Courses will come from the two providers already under nonexclusive agreements. Ratings will be limited to professional course participation, with no patient data.

## Delivery calendar

The team will spend four weeks on exchanges, six on invoice administration, five on the course offer and five on the directory and release preparation. Two engineers and one product lead have twenty weeks reserved. The approved $160,000 envelope covers all work and supplier fees, with a $12,000 reserve.

We will use the company's existing paid-account and email services. There is no equipment purchase and no annual volume commitment. The first release will be limited to the three practice groups that have already agreed to participate in product research.

## Operating the service

A weekday coordinator will resolve account and course-purchase questions. Educational providers retain responsibility for their course materials. The directory will show only information supplied by the practitioner. We will not provide clinical guidance, handle patient referrals or collect consultation records.

The product lead will run monthly interviews with practitioners and practice owners. We will measure released consultation hours, the share of changed sessions filled, and paying practice groups. Revenue will be reported by practice group even when several practitioners hold accounts. A second region needs evidence of improved session coverage and a separate budget.

## First-month work

The lead will prepare the exchange rules and course catalog while engineering configures existing account services. Research participants will receive a monthly briefing. Marketing will prepare personal-account invitations for the dietitians already interviewed, using the same price and terms for all three groups.
