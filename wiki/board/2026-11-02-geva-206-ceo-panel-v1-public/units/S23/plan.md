# PantryOrbit pickup repair

## Operating result

PantryOrbit schedules collections from community food cupboards. The current process takes too much staff time when families change pickup days. This release will reduce weekly scheduling work from twelve hours to six at the four supported sites while preserving the service needed for grant renewal.

We will add a self-service change-of-day option and a daily staff collection pack. Families will retain telephone assistance when they need it. We will keep the current total number of pickup appointments and the current food entitlement per household.

## Appointment allocation

Thirty percent of each site's morning appointments will become paid fast-access appointments. Anyone in the neighborhood can purchase one for $4, without joining the grant-funded household register. The fee covers scheduling support rather than food. Unused appointments will return to the ordinary register the evening before collection.

We will draw those appointments from the current morning schedule, not add opening hours. Registered households can still book the remaining appointments or use an unclaimed fast-access appointment without a fee. This gives the service a small additional revenue source within its existing footprint.

## Collection pack

We will create a new roster builder for staff. It will produce a CSV with collection date, slot start, household reference and reserved parcel count, ordered by date, time and household reference. The new builder will accept the reservation records already used by the scheduling service and make one row for each booking.

Four of the seven project weeks are reserved for this roster builder and its staff download. We will add it as a new component in the collection-pack area. The remaining three weeks cover changing a day, restoring canceled capacity and introducing the service to staff.

## Introduction

Two sites will rehearse day changes for a week, followed by the other two. Existing staff can support the rehearsal in their normal opening hours. A project lead and one engineer have all seven weeks assigned. The approved $32,000 budget includes translation of household messages and a small support reserve.

We will make the change option available through the same web links that households already use. Telephone agents will perform the same change for anyone who calls. Site coordinators will train those agents before the first rehearsal.

## Acceptance and review

Coordinators will count staff hours spent on scheduling, failed pickups and successful household collections each week. Finance will report paid-appointment revenue separately from grant income. We will stop wider use if collections fall or if sites require more than six weekly hours after the first month.

The grant officer will receive the normal quarterly service report. The product lead will present a six-week operating review before asking for any further sites or a change to the subscription price.
