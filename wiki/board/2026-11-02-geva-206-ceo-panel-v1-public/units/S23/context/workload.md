# Staff observations and budget

Staff spend twelve hours each week across the four sites on scheduling. About eight hours involve canceling and rebooking a changed day. A two-week manual rehearsal of direct changes reduced that work to two hours. The remaining four hours cover ordinary questions and collection preparation.

Existing telephone agents can handle the remaining calls during normal shifts. Households already use the same web links for initial bookings, so no new account or separate purchase is needed for a day change. Existing translators can prepare the messages within the stated reserve.

The engineer is assigned for seven weeks and the project lead can train all four sites. Reusing the current roster download and adding day changes is estimated at three weeks. There are no new service charges or annual commitments.
