# Funding conditions

The current grant pays for all opening hours, scheduling support and food parcels at the four sites. It requires every appointment in those funded opening hours to remain available without a fee to households on the register. Those appointments cannot be reserved for unregistered customers, even if the payment is described as a service fee rather than a food charge.

The grant officer reviews appointment allocation as part of renewal. A paid reservation pool within existing opening hours would make the sites ineligible for renewal. Grant income represents 81 percent of the program's annual operating funds.

Sites may run separately funded evening services for other residents, but none currently has staff or a budget for evening opening. The approved repair budget concerns only the existing morning service.
