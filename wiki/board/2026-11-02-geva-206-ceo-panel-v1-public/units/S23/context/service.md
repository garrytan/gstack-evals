# Sites and households

PantryOrbit serves four community food cupboards through a regional support grant. It provides scheduling software and telephone booking assistance. Sites distribute food to households on a grant-funded register. Households may use web booking or telephone agents at the same price, which is zero.

Morning slots are popular with households that rely on the community transport route. All sites currently open only in the morning. The schedule has sufficient total food and appointments for registered households, but not enough spare morning capacity to add another customer group at the current opening hours.

A self-service day change has been requested by households and site staff. The current system requires an agent to cancel and rebook each change. Two sites can rehearse it with their present staffing.
