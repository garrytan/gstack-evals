# Recruitment access
Three trade-training partners have signed no-fee distribution agreements covering monthly sessions. Their next sessions include employees from thirty employers not using Copperfinch. Those employers have between four and fourteen eligible staff each. The partners can distribute an opt-in invitation but cannot authorize an employer purchase or disclose attendance lists.

The employers use the same reimbursement process as the manual-trial firms. Each has a named finance lead reachable through an employee introduction. None requires a reseller, central group approval or a bank switch. A normal monthly service agreement is sufficient after the finance lead approves.

The partners accept a maximum of 360 activations for this campaign and will remove the invitation when Copperfinch closes enrollment. No advertising prepayment or future exclusivity is required.
