# Partner-session trial
A manual trial ran at trade-training sessions with twelve eligible employers represented by seventy-two employees. Employees collected their own receipts in temporary folders and sent a standard introduction to their finance lead. All twelve finance leads accepted an introductory call. Ten began a trial and eight became paying customers, with all eight completing their second reimbursement cycle.

Finance leads said the employee introduction gave them a real example of the current paperwork and a named internal sponsor. None treated the employee as the buyer. Each purchase went through the finance lead and the normal employer agreement. No employer adopted from an anonymous receipt upload alone.

Direct finance-manager advertisements in the same period produced fewer qualified calls at a higher acquisition cost. The team has retained that channel for later comparison, but the current partner route is sufficient for the approved cohort.
