# Employer product
Copperfinch serves 180 small field-service employers. Finance managers buy it, set policies and approve reimbursements. Employees submit receipts only after their employer opens an account. The current product has no way to hold a worker's receipts before that purchase, and employers receive messy email attachments during evaluation.

The new commercial objective is to add twelve employers who complete a second paid reimbursement cycle. Existing employers have 94% annual retention and stable reimbursement accuracy. No change to the approval or payment process is needed for this objective.

The pocket can reuse image capture and accounting exports. Its distinct work is personal storage, permissioned transfer and an invitation from a worker to an authorized finance lead. The paid product's pricing and processing margin remain unchanged.
