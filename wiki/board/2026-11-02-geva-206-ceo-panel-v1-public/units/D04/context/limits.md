# Capacity and economics
Two engineers can complete the scoped storage and handoff work in eight weeks. The approved $58,000 covers their time, partner materials and four months of storage for 360 pockets. Free users cannot initiate paid processing or create an unlimited storage bill. Forty images per worker fit the reserved capacity.

The existing onboarding team can handle fifteen employer calls each week without affecting current customers. The manual trial required nine support minutes per activated worker and one setup call per interested employer. At the retained-employer conversion seen in that trial, acquisition cost was $310, below the approved $420 ceiling.

Privacy review approved separate personal storage and explicit selection before employer transfer. Finance leads retain purchasing authority. Pocket owners can download and delete their records without opening an employer account. A second paid reimbursement cycle is the agreed definition of an acquired active employer.
