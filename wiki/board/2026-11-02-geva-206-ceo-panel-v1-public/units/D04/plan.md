# Copperfinch receipt pockets

## The next customer path
Copperfinch sells expense approval and reimbursement software to small field-service employers. We will add a free receipt pocket that an employee can use before their employer opens an account. The pocket will store receipt images, dates and amounts, then prepare a clean handoff to the employer's finance lead.

The commercial goal is twelve new paying employers within twelve weeks of release, each processing a second monthly reimbursement cycle. We will charge employers the existing $85 monthly base price plus their normal usage fees. Employees will not pay, and a receipt pocket will not authorize reimbursement or create an employer obligation.

## Start with employees
Our first acquisition campaign will address technicians and field staff, not finance managers. Trade-training partners will distribute the pocket invitation at their monthly sessions. We will make no separate finance-manager advertising purchase during the campaign. A worker can keep up to forty receipts free and invite one finance lead to review an export.

The invitation will identify the employee and ask the finance lead whether to open an employer trial. An employee cannot subscribe for an employer, upload a staff roster or set a reimbursement policy. If the finance lead declines, the worker keeps a personal download and can close the pocket. We will not send repeated sales messages to a declined contact.

## Employer handoff
An interested finance lead receives a fifteen-minute setup call with our existing onboarding team. That lead confirms purchasing authority, selects the expense policy and approves a paid subscription after a thirty-day trial. The employee then chooses which receipts to transfer into the employer account. Personal receipts stay private.

The release includes capture, duplicate detection, a reviewable export and the invitation handoff. We will reuse the existing employer approval and payment workflow. It does not include employee payments, personal budgeting, employer credit or a new card program. Finance leads can use the current accounting export without changing banks.

## Delivery and exposure
Two engineers have eight weeks allocated. The first three weeks cover personal storage and capture. The next three cover export and consent-based transfer. The final two cover the invitation handoff, support preparation and deletion testing. The approved $58,000 budget includes four months of free storage and partner recruitment.

We will invite at most 360 employees from thirty employers. Employer accounts remain subject to ordinary finance approval; there is no automatic conversion. The partner sessions are opt-in and do not share employee lists with us. We will close enrollment at the cap even if partner demand is higher. The onboarding team has reserved fifteen employer setup slots per week.

## What determines the next release
We will record the complete path from activated employee to invited finance lead, authorized trial, paid employer and second reimbursement cycle. Twelve paid employers completing that cycle is the success threshold. Free pocket count will explain reach but will not authorize a larger campaign.

Expansion also requires acquisition cost below $420 per retained employer, no unapproved transfer of personal receipts and support within the reserved team hours. We will review results after twelve weeks. If the threshold is missed, existing paid employers will continue normally, while free enrollment stops and pocket owners receive an export and thirty days to decide whether to retain their records.
