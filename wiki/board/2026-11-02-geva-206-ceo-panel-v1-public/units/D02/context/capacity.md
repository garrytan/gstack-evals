# Setup and operating calculation

Eight tutors have signed twelve-week employment schedules totaling 240 productive hours weekly. The full course needs 174 hours of instruction and 36 hours of preparation, learner contact and notes each week. That leaves thirty hours for absence cover and variations in lesson pace. The consortium has two substitute tutors available under a paid standby agreement included in the budget.

The print-and-post provider has reserved capacity for all 180 weekly packs and accessible formats. Its price is fixed for the course. Telephone minutes and classroom provision are contracted for the same volume. The six-week setup estimate includes rehearsal of route changes and a full sample mailing, with one week reserved for corrections.

Total expected cost is $156,000 including a ten percent contingency, against the $198,000 signed order. The advance pays setup costs; later payments fall before the relevant payroll dates. Curriculum content is licensed for all three delivery routes. Shared course records reduce repeated tutor preparation rather than requiring three independent teaching teams. No outside fundraising or unapproved hire is needed to complete this first order.
