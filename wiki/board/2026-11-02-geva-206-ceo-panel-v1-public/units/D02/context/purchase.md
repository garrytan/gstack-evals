# Consortium order

The Brevin Learning Consortium coordinates adult education across five rural districts. It has signed a $198,000 order for a twelve-week service for 180 learners. The order is conditional on classroom, telephone and posted-paper participation all being ready on the first teaching day. A classroom-only or telephone-only service does not meet the purchased specification. The consortium has authorized a thirty percent setup payment and named a purchasing officer with full authority.

The consortium operates the only funded adult reading program in these districts this year. It selected Hearthfen because learners can change teaching route during the course while keeping the same tutor record. The consortium provides enrollment teams, classrooms and an independent assessor. Hearthfen supplies tutors, course administration and learning materials.

The contract caps enrollment at 180. It allows the course to decline additional learners and carries no obligation to open another district. Acceptance includes operation of all three routes, assessment completion and accessible formats. It does not require a mobile application, accredited qualification or a new content library.
