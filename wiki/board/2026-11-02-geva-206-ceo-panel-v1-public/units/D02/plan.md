# Hearthfen reading service

## The first contract
Hearthfen is a new adult literacy service for rural learning districts. We will deliver one twelve-week reading course through classroom meetings, scheduled telephone lessons and posted paper packs. Our first customer is the Brevin Learning Consortium, whose signed order covers 180 learners across all three routes. The first-year goal is to deliver that order profitably and improve independently assessed reading completion, not to launch a general consumer course business.

## A single course in three forms
All three teaching routes will be available on the first teaching day. A learner can move between them without starting again. The lesson sequence, tutor notes and progress record will be shared, so a tutor can continue from the last completed activity. We will include the paper pack and telephone option in every enrollment, even when a learner begins in a classroom.

The course will cover practical reading for work schedules, household forms and community notices. It will not issue professional qualifications or provide legal or financial advice. Tutors will use the approved teaching material and may adjust pace rather than inventing a separate course for each learner. Learners can ask for large print or a different contact time at enrollment.

## Delivery preparation
We will prepare all three routes in a six-week setup period. Two curriculum specialists will adapt the existing licensed content. Three engineers will build scheduling, tutor notes and progress reports using hosted services. The operations lead will rehearse pack delivery and telephone scheduling with the consortium before enrollment opens.

The consortium will recruit learners through its existing district teams and will provide classroom rooms. Hearthfen will employ eight tutors and contract the print-and-post service already selected in procurement. The tutors' committed weekly hours cover both instruction and administration at the full 180-learner volume. Staff will call anyone whose pack does not arrive and arrange an alternative before the relevant lesson.

## Commercial boundaries
The consortium pays $198,000 for the course, with thirty percent due at setup and the balance paid in scheduled stages. Our full delivery budget is $156,000, including staff, materials, postage, accessible formats, support and a ten percent contingency. We will not take additional district orders until the first course has finished. Unused learner places can be filled only during the first two weeks and within the purchased total.

## Completion and the next decision
An assessor appointed by the consortium will measure reading at entry and after week twelve. We will report completion, reading improvement and results by teaching route, including learners who move between routes. Payment acceptance requires all three routes to have operated and at least 75 percent of learners to finish the final assessment. A second district offer will require those conditions, a positive delivery margin and no unresolved accessibility complaint. Otherwise we will repair the course within the current contract before expanding.
