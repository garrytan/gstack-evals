# Trial resources

The project has one engineer for five weeks and an operations lead for enrollment and financial reconciliation. The existing payment service supports funded balances and returns. The $26,000 budget includes two months of support, and the employer deposits remain separate from that budget.

At the capped volume, recurring support and balance administration cost about $900 per month. The five administration fees total $1,250 per month. Employer class spending is paid from the deposits and creates no additional subsidy obligation for StepBirch.

Employers currently spend an average of 25 minutes per employee per month processing allowance claims. HR will measure that time again during the trial. Booking records can identify repeat use in later weeks, and the renewal review includes both that result and employer willingness to pay.
