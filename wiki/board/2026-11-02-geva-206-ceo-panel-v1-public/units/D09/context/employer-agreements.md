# Signed employer trial terms

All five employers have signed eight-week trial agreements through their authorized purchasing teams. Each has approved a $250 monthly administration fee and deposited $1,500 for class credits. Each employer's HR owner has approved the eligibility list and a $100 per-person trial limit.

The agreements explicitly appoint the existing employee activity captains to run staff enrollment. Purchasing teams asked not to receive a second launch campaign: their part is complete. HR will send captains the approved group link and can answer eligibility questions through the normal internal contact.

Each employer will receive the agreed aggregate monthly financial report. The approved terms cover the limited booking information needed for allowance administration. Renewal and any larger rollout require a new decision by the same purchasing team.
