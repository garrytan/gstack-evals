# Employee route

Each employer already has an activity captain who circulates weekly class suggestions to colleagues. Last quarter these messages reached 91 percent of current StepBirch users at the five firms. Employees recognize the captains and use their links for ordinary class bookings.

The HR owners selected this route after an earlier benefits email received little attention. They have approved the exact invitation text, the group-link process and time for captains to answer first questions. Captains will not collect payments or approve employer spending.

A rehearsal with ten employees confirmed that they could connect their current accounts without contacting purchasing or changing a provider. Employees can decline the option and continue using StepBirch normally. The full trial population is capped by the signed eligibility lists.
