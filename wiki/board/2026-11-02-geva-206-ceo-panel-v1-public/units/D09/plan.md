# StepBirch employer class credits

## Offer

StepBirch currently lets adults book local exercise classes. We will add an employer-funded credit option so employees can use a workplace allowance without filing a separate expense claim. The employer remains the paying customer for this option. Employees will use their existing class-booking accounts.

The first release covers five employers and the class providers already in StepBirch. It will not add gyms, sell treatment services or change class prices. Employers will pay the agreed monthly administration fee and fund their own credit balances in advance. StepBirch will not lend money or subsidize attendance.

## Getting employees started

We will introduce the option through employee activity captains. Each captain will receive a group link and a short message to circulate to colleagues. Employees will follow that link, connect their existing account and see the workplace credit available for eligible classes.

We will use these internal employee messages rather than send a separate launch campaign to employer purchasing teams. The first support contact for a colleague will be the activity captain, with StepBirch's existing weekday team handling account or credit questions. No one needs to change class provider or submit a personal reimbursement request.

## Credit handling

A booking will show the employee's available workplace credit and any personal amount due before confirmation. A canceled class returns the credit under the current cancellation rules. When an employer's funded balance is exhausted, the employer option pauses; personal bookings remain available at the usual price.

Employers will receive a monthly total of credit used and the remaining balance. The report will not include personal activity descriptions beyond the minimum booking information already approved for allowance administration. We will keep ordinary personal bookings outside the employer report.

## Delivery

One engineer and an operations lead have five weeks assigned. The work covers workplace account linking, funded balances and the monthly employer total. We will reuse existing booking, cancellation and payment services. The $26,000 project budget includes captain briefings and the first two months of support.

We will open to ten employees per employer in week four and to the remaining eligible staff in week five after credit totals reconcile. Employers can add money but cannot create an unfunded balance. The operations lead will check each employer balance before opening its group link.

## Results and next decision

The trial lasts eight weeks. We want at least half of invited existing users to book an eligible class with workplace credit and at least 40 percent to do so again in a later week. We will also compare allowance administration time with the previous claims process.

We will extend the offer only if the employers renew, repeated use meets the target and support stays within the approved allowance. Employers can stop after the trial; unused funds will be returned and personal booking accounts will continue unchanged.
