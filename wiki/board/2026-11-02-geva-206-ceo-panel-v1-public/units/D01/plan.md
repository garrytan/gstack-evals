# CrumbQuay: first route to bakeries

## Four-week brief

CrumbQuay will be a new software line for neighborhood bakeries that sell leftover batches to nearby lunch counters. The immediate project is to establish whether our wholesaler introduction route can reach the bakery managers who decide how leftovers are sold. We will build a small invitation and demonstration service for that project.

This is a four-week, $8,000 release. It will not open a paid marketplace or change how a bakery disposes of food. A working invitation service and a short batch-listing demonstration will let a manager see the offer and arrange a conversation. Actual batch transactions remain with the existing trial coordinator.

## Product work

The invitation will show the participating wholesaler and the bakery's contact, then offer available meeting times. The demonstration will show how a manager can list an unsold batch, choose an existing lunch-counter buyer and see the offered collection time. It will use sample batches supplied for demonstration, not live inventory.

The service will accept only bakery managers introduced by the two participating wholesalers. We will not add buyer recruitment, public listings, payment collection or delivery booking in this project. A coordinator can answer every meeting request within one weekday.

## Success for this release

We will complete at least eighteen meetings with verified bakery managers who have authority to choose a surplus-sales service. The cost per completed qualified meeting must remain below $45, excluding the fixed product build. A booking that is canceled, unattended or made by someone without purchasing authority will not count.

The project report will show invitations delivered, completed qualified meetings and the direct cost of those meetings. We will not score this release on food sold or waste avoided. The report's decision is whether this introduction route is usable for the next commercial test.

## Schedule and people

During week one, one engineer will connect the invitation page and existing appointment service. The coordinator will prepare demonstration batches with the wholesalers. Week two will cover a rehearsal with two managers, followed by invitations in weeks three and four.

The wholesalers have agreed to introduce up to 120 managers at a fixed fee within the budget. An engineer has five working days reserved. The coordinator has capacity for 25 meetings. No annual contract or minimum future transaction volume is attached.

## What follows

A successful result permits the product lead to request a separately budgeted paid transaction trial. It does not authorize a marketplace build. That next proposal must measure seller proceeds, completed collections and food left unsold against the existing process. If the route does not meet the meeting and cost targets, the invitation service will close and the unspent budget will return to the company.
