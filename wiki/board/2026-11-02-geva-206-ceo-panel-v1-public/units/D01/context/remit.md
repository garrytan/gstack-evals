# Approved first stage

PineBowl makes small business administration tools. It is considering CrumbQuay as a new line. The long-term purpose would be to improve bakeries' recovery on unsold food without increasing staff work. The company has not approved a marketplace build.

The current approval covers one question: can two wholesale suppliers introduce us to bakery managers with buying authority at a manageable cost? The authorized project is four weeks with a total budget of $8,000. Completed qualified meetings and their direct acquisition cost are the required results.

Food waste, seller proceeds and repeat use belong to a subsequent paid transaction trial. No decision to build or expand that service can be made from meeting counts alone. The product lead must return with a separate budget and an outcome-measurement proposal.
