# People and available services

An engineer has five days reserved and can use the company's existing appointment and email services. The demonstration needs no transactions, payments or live food records. The coordinator can conduct 25 meetings across four weeks within her assigned hours.

The company has permission to use the manual trial's sample batch descriptions in the demonstration. It will not display household or personal dietary information. The lunch counters in the manual work can continue their current arrangements while this project runs.

Finance will separate fixed build costs from the variable cost of gaining a completed qualified meeting. A qualified meeting requires attendance by the named manager and confirmation that the manager can choose the service. A mere click, registration or calendar booking is not a completed meeting.
