# Wholesaler introductions

Two local ingredient wholesalers have signed a four-week introduction agreement. Together they can reach 120 independent bakeries in the selected district. Their account records name the manager who sets purchasing and surplus-sale policy at each bakery. Managers can decline without any effect on supply.

The fixed introduction fee is $420. There is no exclusivity, renewal or future purchase obligation. The fee and meeting-related coordinator time can remain below the agreed $45 per completed meeting if eighteen managers attend. The $8,000 budget separately covers the limited product work.

Each wholesaler will send the invitations itself. It has approval to make business introductions to these contacts. PineBowl will receive contact details only when a manager requests a meeting. No customer list is being bought.
