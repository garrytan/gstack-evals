# Earlier manual work

A coordinator ran a six-week manual service with six bakery managers and nine lunch counters. Managers listed leftovers after the morning rush, existing lunch-counter buyers collected them, and bakeries recorded proceeds and unsold weight. Both parties completed 89 of 94 agreed collections.

The bakery managers recovered more money than through their existing end-of-day discount table and spent no additional staff time overall. Buyers wanted the same collection window each day. All six managers said they would consider a paid trial using those terms.

The managers in that work came through the founder's personal contacts. Those contacts are exhausted and do not overlap the wholesale suppliers' lists. The next question is access to new decision makers, not whether the manual batch process can work in the original group.
