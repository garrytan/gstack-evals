# Telephone agreements

The legacy vendor charges $4,200 per month for its number, forwarding and a result spreadsheet. Its terms allow cancellation after a month's notice. Once returned to the provider, the number cannot be reclaimed. Keeping the contract for another year would cost $50,400.

The practices' central-number agreement continues regardless of cancellation of the legacy vendor. It has sufficient capacity for both streams and no additional charge for the combined volume. Receptionists can answer and return calls through it without the legacy account.

Finance has approved the $24,000 transition budget and up to twelve weeks of overlap. It also approved a small extension if a practice needs more time to update materials. There is no minimum remaining contract term and no cancellation penalty.
