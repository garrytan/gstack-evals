# Capacity and service review

The existing central queue has been measured at three times the combined peak call volume. Its reception schedule covers all advertised callback hours. One engineer can add the shared result record in two weeks; training and templates require another two. Assigned reception contacts can attend training without reducing phone coverage.

Current reports include completed confirmations, missed callbacks and receptionist handling minutes. A manual list of appointments can preserve the same measures during a service interruption. No clinical information is needed for the report.

The account lead will keep forwarding open if call counts or confirmations fail the agreed conditions. Following cancellation, the central line remains available for every patient and the manual list remains available for staff. The observation period is funded before notice is submitted.
