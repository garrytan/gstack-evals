# Contact audit

All current public websites and directory listings show the central number. The old number remains on a small stock of printed appointment cards and three reminder templates. Each practice has agreed to replace those materials before the observation period.

The vendor can provide daily counts of direct calls to its old number, excluding test calls. Those counts will allow staff to verify that no patient still relies on it. The account lead has access to every practice's material checklist.

The practices approved permanent removal of the old route after four call-free weeks and stable confirmation results. They do not need the old number to be recoverable: a staffed central line, appointment lists and direct callbacks form their agreed continuity process.
