# Current callback service

CareBrindle supports six physiotherapy practices. Older reminder templates use a vendor-owned callback number. Newer templates use the practices' central number. Both routes reach the same reception staff during the same weekday hours, but the vendor sends a separate spreadsheet of results.

Staff spend about ten hours per week matching that spreadsheet to the central queue. A shared callback record removes that work without changing who makes appointment decisions. Patient interviews favored a single telephone contact and did not request an application.

The central number has operated for two years and belongs to the practices under a separate agreement. The central queue handled all calls during a two-week legacy-vendor outage without missed confirmations or extra reception hours.
