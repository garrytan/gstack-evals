# CareBrindle callback consolidation

## Outcome

CareBrindle runs appointment reminders for neighborhood physiotherapy practices. We will repair the callback service so patients and reception teams use one contact number and one queue. The target is to cut receptionist time spent reconciling callbacks by half while maintaining successful appointment confirmation.

The service will retain telephone access. Patients do not need a smartphone or an online account. Reception teams will continue to handle scheduling decisions themselves. We will not change appointment eligibility, prices or the hours in which a practice offers treatment.

## Contact route

All reminder messages will carry the practices' current central callback number. The existing central queue will show the appointment reference and the last callback attempt. A receptionist will record the result once, and the reminder service will use that result when deciding whether another attempt is needed.

After the introduction period, we will cancel the legacy callback-number contract and release that number to its provider. We will remove it from the contact list and close the old vendor account. The central number and queue will then be the sole route for reminder callbacks.

## Introduction sequence

The first two weeks cover the shared callback record and reception training. We will then update printed cards, practice recordings and reminder templates. Reception teams will check the materials at all six practices before the observation period begins.

For eight weeks, the old number will forward callers to the central queue and the provider will supply daily call counts. The account lead will close the legacy contract only after four consecutive weeks with no calls to the old number and confirmation from every practice that its materials are current. If either condition is unmet, forwarding will continue within the reserved transition budget.

## Continuity

Receptionists will retain a daily appointment list and the central telephone line. If the shared callback record is unavailable, they will record call results on that list and reconcile them when service returns. The central line contract is separate from the legacy vendor account and will remain active.

A receptionist and a technical contact are assigned at every practice. The central queue already supports the combined call volume. We will keep the present weekday response hours and publish no new response-time promise.

## Delivery and review

One engineer and the account lead have four weeks assigned, followed by the observation period. The project budget is $24,000, including temporary forwarding and training. No new recurring supplier is needed.

The account lead will compare receptionist minutes per completed confirmation, missed callbacks and confirmed appointments against the previous eight weeks. Contract closure needs stable appointment confirmation and no rise in missed callbacks as well as the contact-route checks. We will review the result again one month after closure.
