# PorterFen six-week field trial

## Question and decision
PorterFen is a new luggage-transfer service between small inns in Orrel Vale and the valley rail station. We will test whether round-trip leisure guests will buy a reliable $17 transfer rather than carry bags on the local walking routes. The trial has a fixed six-week term and a maximum of 120 travelers. It is not a permanent launch.

The decision is whether to fund a paid seasonal service at the regular price. We will proceed only if at least 35% of enrolled travelers buy the return transfer at $17, at least 98% of accepted transfers meet their promised train window, and measured recurring cost stays at or below $11 per transfer. Results below any threshold end the offer after existing bookings are fulfilled.

## Trial price
Each enrolled traveler can buy one introductory outbound transfer for $5. The same traveler can then buy one return transfer for $17. Both prices are shown before enrollment. The introductory rate ends with the six-week trial and cannot be renewed or used for a second outbound transfer. No automatic subscription or later charge is attached to it.

We will fund the difference between the introductory charge and the carrier's $11 transfer fee from the approved $720 offer allowance. The allowance covers all 120 introductory transfers even if no return transfer sells. We will close enrollment at the traveler limit or the end date, whichever comes first. Staff cannot issue additional introductory bookings or extend the rate.

## Booking and custody
Six inns will offer the service to guests who have both arrival and return train dates. Guests choose from published collection slots and pay on a simple hosted booking page. The inn labels each bag, checks the guest's booking reference, and records handover to the carrier. The carrier delivers to the staffed station counter and records receipt. No unattended pavement drop is permitted.

The carrier has reserved twelve transfer slots a day. The page will close a slot when the daily allocation is full; it will not take payment for an unconfirmed place. The booking team will group nearby inns within the carrier's existing circuit. Guests may cancel until the preceding evening for a full refund. The carrier's contract covers loss, delay, and the station counter arrangement.

## Preparation and staffing
The coordinator will spend the first week setting up booking records, training the inn desks, and rehearsing handovers. Operations begin only after the station counter confirms each route and the carrier completes two end-to-end practice runs. A part-time coordinator will cover the published service hours, with the carrier's dispatcher handling live vehicle issues. We will offer no late-night service or extra towns.

## Close and review
The full trial budget is $6,000, including the offer allowance, coordinator time, booking setup, training, and contingency. Every week finance will reconcile transfers, cash, and the remaining allowance. We will publish an internal result for every enrolled traveler, including those who decline the full-price return. At the end we will close the booking page, fulfill accepted bookings, and write the seasonal investment decision. There is no automatic extension while that decision is pending.
