# Trial authorization

The founders approved a $6,000 experiment allocation. It consists of $720 for introductory transfers, $2,400 for the coordinator, $1,800 for booking setup and training, and $1,080 contingency. None of the allocation depends on future sales. The introductory allowance is calculated as 120 transfers times the $6 difference between the $5 customer charge and the $11 carrier fee. The founders will not replenish it during this test.

The carrier fee includes all vehicle, driver, insurance, station handover, and payment-processing costs for an individual transfer. It is fixed at $11 for the trial and for a subsequent season of up to 1,000 transfers if PorterFen chooses to proceed. Ongoing booking administration would add $2 per transfer in a seasonal service. The $17 regular price therefore covers the documented recurring costs. A seasonal service would have a separate setup and staffing approval.

The trial's learning goal is paid return-trip purchase at the regular price, with reliable handover. Free interest, introductory take-up, and website visits do not authorize a seasonal commitment. The approval requires all three stated continuation conditions and a new decision after the trial closes.
