# Local travel research

PorterFen's founders interviewed 42 guests staying at six valley inns. Thirty-one wanted to walk between the station and inn but took a taxi because of luggage. A taxi with baggage costs $29 each way. There is no shared luggage-only transfer currently available from these inns, and the station counter accepts bags only from contracted carriers, not directly from guests.

Twenty-six interviewees said they would consider a $17 luggage-only transfer. Their answers were not purchase commitments. The founders want actual paid decisions before committing to a season of service. The inn group can reach 120 eligible round-trip guests over six weeks through booking confirmations. Their return dates fall inside the trial window. Invitations will show both the introductory and regular prices.

The inns have signed a trial referral agreement with no commission or exclusive term. They can label bags at their existing desks without new staffing. Their managers have approved the daily handover procedure and can direct guests to the booking page. Guests remain free to carry their bags or book a taxi instead.
