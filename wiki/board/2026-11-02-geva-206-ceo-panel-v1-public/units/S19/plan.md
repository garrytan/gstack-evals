# TempoHearth response service

## Decision

We will repair the coach-question service for paying exercise members. Members use it to ask about their weekly activity plan and arrange the next coaching conversation. The purpose of this release is to reduce unanswered questions and keep members through their next renewal.

The release will add a shared coach inbox, a named owner for each question and an automated receipt. Members will see when a coach has taken responsibility. Coaches can book a follow-up conversation through the existing appointment service. The scope does not include new exercise programs or more kinds of advice.

## Service offer

Every paid member will receive a human coach reply within two hours, at any time on any day. The promise will appear in renewal messages and the service description when we release the inbox. The same promise applies during weekends and holidays.

Our current three coaches will run the service. We will retain their existing shifts and use their spare appointment hours for written replies. The coach lead will review open questions at the beginning and end of each shift. We will not buy external cover or change the approved staffing budget.

## How we will score the release

The principal success number will be the share of submitted questions that receive the automated receipt within one minute. We will aim for 99.5 percent, compared with the current receipt rate. The receipt confirms that a question has reached the shared queue and tells the member that a coach owns the next step.

The weekly report will show total questions, one-minute receipts and the percentage meeting that target. The release counts as successful after four consecutive weeks above target. We will use the same definition for all membership types.

## Work plan

One engineer has four weeks for the inbox and receipts. The appointment link is already part of the member service and needs no new supplier. The coach lead has two rehearsal sessions reserved, using ordinary member questions with the current staff.

A product coordinator will update membership messages and prepare coach instructions. The approved project budget is $18,000. Delivery will cover the current 180 members only. New membership acquisition stays at its existing pace and is not part of this project.

## Daily operation

Questions remain with a named coach until a reply or a booked conversation resolves the request. A member can decline a proposed appointment and keep the written conversation open. The service will address routine coaching questions only; it will continue directing requests for medical treatment to the member's own clinician.

The coach lead owns the queue and will present the four-week service report to the membership director. We will keep the existing price and cancellation terms.
