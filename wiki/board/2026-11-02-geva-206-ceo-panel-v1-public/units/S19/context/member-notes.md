# Member conversations

Twelve recent cancellation interviews mentioned questions going unanswered for several days. Members could obtain an automated email confirmation but did not know which coach would answer. Ten said a reliable next-business-day human reply would have met their needs.

Staff tested a named-owner list for two weeks. It reduced lost questions during weekday shifts without adding hours. Members liked follow-up appointments when the coach actually offered a suitable time. They did not request night coaching or holiday coverage.

Members distinguish a receipt from an answer. Their recurring complaint is not uncertainty about whether an email arrived; it is a lack of useful human follow-through. The membership director's stated objective is fewer unresolved requests and better renewal among people who ask questions.
