# TempoHearth membership

TempoHearth provides ordinary exercise planning and short coach conversations for 180 adult members. It does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. Members pay $48 per month. The current margin can support written coaching at the observed average question volume.

Members can already book follow-up appointments. Written questions arrive through a shared email address, and individual coaches sometimes miss questions they expect another coach to take. A named queue owner is the agreed remedy from staff and customer interviews.

The four-week project has an engineer and coordinator assigned. Existing appointment services support links from a queue. The company has approved $18,000 for delivery and no ongoing staffing increase. There is no dependency on new acquisition.
