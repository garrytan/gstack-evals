# Recent service figures

| Month | One-minute receipt share | Questions resolved within next business day | Renewal among members asking questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 95% | 82% | 91% |
| Second | 98% | 64% | 84% |
| Third | 99% | 51% | 77% |

Receipts are generated before a coach sees a message. They contain the question number and the expected next step but no coaching content. A question with a receipt can remain unanswered indefinitely.

The existing reports can count human replies, unresolved questions and subsequent renewal without additional data collection. The queue volume stayed between 110 and 125 questions per month over this period. Receipt delivery improved after an email-service update; coaching shifts did not change.
