# Coach coverage

The three coaches each work Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 in the members' local time zone. Those are their entire paid shifts. No coach is scheduled overnight, on weekends or on holidays. Coaches have not agreed to be on call outside those hours.

Together they have 22 spare hours a week after booked conversations. Current question volume takes about 15 coach-hours a week, so a weekday written service fits. The members are in a single time zone, and the existing appointment service advertises weekday hours.

About 30 percent of questions arrive outside the scheduled shifts, including roughly ten each Saturday. The staffing budget has no allocation for external cover, overtime or additional hiring. The lead can change assignments within shifts but cannot extend paid hours.
