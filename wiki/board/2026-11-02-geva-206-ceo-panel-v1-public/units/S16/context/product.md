# VisitThyme today

VisitThyme has 420 active care circles. A circle organizer books visits, invites relatives and approves volunteer expenses. Relatives can see booked and completed visits. Current update messages are short text entries without a monthly collection.

Organizers have asked for optional photo collections as a pleasant addition. All circles already control who can see visit information. The membership service can support the proposed organizer-approved journal without a new commercial agreement.

The next release budget is $28,000. One engineer, a content lead and the customer lead have time assigned. Existing reimbursement service fees are covered by the present subscription price. The subscription is profitable at the current use level, and no price increase is needed for a small journal.
