# County care cooperative

The cooperative lists one care-circle software provider for the county. VisitThyme holds that seat. The cooperative does not handle care delivery or account support; it gives families an introduction link that opens ordinary VisitThyme enrollment.

Directory referrals supplied 34 percent of new paid circles over the past year. Their renewal rate matches that of directly acquired circles. Membership costs $350 annually, and maintaining the connector takes about one staff hour per quarter. There is no separate customer price or special welcome process.

A departing provider's seat passes immediately to the next applicant. A former member cannot apply again for five years. The cooperative has confirmed that it will retain VisitThyme's seat at the current fee if membership continues. The current agreement imposes no obligation to send notice this quarter.
