# Departing circles

Of the last 60 circles, 19 did not renew at three months. Seventeen of those 19 said volunteers stopped accepting visits because expense repayments arrived late. The median repayment took 18 days, largely because organizers received only a monthly expense summary.

None of the 19 said the lack of photographs or family updates influenced departure. Fifteen had frequent text updates and reported that relatives felt well informed. Their relatives still struggled to find a willing volunteer for the following month.

A separate test with eight circles sent the existing expense summary weekly. Repayments fell to a median of five days and volunteers accepted more repeat visits. The same reimbursement service can accept weekly submissions. The journal requests came mostly from established circles that have renewed for more than a year.
