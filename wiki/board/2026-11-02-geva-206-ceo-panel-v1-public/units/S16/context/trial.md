# Trial arrangements

Twelve current organizers have agreed to rehearse a journal. They will provide only updates and photographs they already have permission to share with their circle. The content lead will check that the organizer can select recipients and approve contributions. Public sharing is not part of the offer.

The customer lead can collect renewal, filled-visit and volunteer-participation figures from normal account reports. Leaving circles already receive a voluntary exit interview. The previous 60-circle cohort has comparable visit frequency and geography.

Direct enrollment is working and can take all new accounts at the current volume. Keeping the cooperative introduction link also requires no change to billing, membership approval or support. No supplier has made directory membership a condition of the journal release.
