# VisitThyme family updates

## Why now

VisitThyme helps families arrange volunteer visits for an adult relative. We want to improve the share of care circles that renew after their first three months. The next release will add a family journal to the existing visit calendar, making it easier to see the good moments that volunteers and relatives create.

Renewal is the main result. We will compare the next 60 new circles with the previous 60, controlling for the number of visits booked. We will also count filled visits and volunteer participation. The customer lead will interview every circle that leaves during the trial.

## The journal

Volunteers will be able to attach a short written update and an optional photograph after a visit. Relatives can collect selected updates into a monthly family album. A circle organizer will decide who can contribute and which relatives receive the album. A text-only update will always be available.

The first release is limited to updates, monthly collections and organizer approval. We will not change volunteer expense handling or the existing reimbursement service. Those remain available under the current terms. The journal will be included in the circle subscription without a price increase.

## Enrollment administration

At release, we will close the county care-cooperative connector and surrender VisitThyme's seat in its referral directory. Existing circles will keep their accounts and subscriptions. New circles will enroll through our direct website, giving us one enrollment route and one set of welcome materials.

The account team will remove the cooperative option from its enrollment queue on launch day. We will send the cooperative the standard membership cancellation notice and use our direct enrollment materials for all future inquiries.

## Work and release

One engineer and a content lead have six weeks assigned. We will use the existing circle membership service and email delivery. Twelve current circles have agreed to a two-week rehearsal using only material they choose to share. The customer lead will confirm each organizer's sharing choices before opening the trial.

The budget is $28,000 plus current staff time. No additional staffing is required at the planned volume. Support will answer ordinary journal questions within the existing weekday service window.

## Review after three months

We will retain the journal if renewal improves without fewer filled visits or increased volunteer departures. We will not extend the feature to public posting or cross-circle discovery. After the three-month review, the customer lead can propose a separate release for any additional family requests. Until then, all other visit-booking and expense functions stay as they are.
