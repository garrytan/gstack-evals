# Distribution notes

HearthPost sells placements in newsletters for family carers. It has supplied audited audience counts, but neither our company nor the family association has sold a subscription through those newsletters. The association's member referral route is separate and can fill the first 80 places.

The agency offers two purchase routes. One is a $900, three-week placement test across two newsletters. It has available dates before the annual reservation deadline and uses the same audience segments and placement format as the annual package. The other is a twelve-month reservation for $60,000, payable on signature with no early termination.

The marketing lead's outstanding question is how many readers will pay $14 rather than only request a free guide. A completed test can measure both paid conversions and cancellations. HearthPost will hold the annual price until that test's results are available.
