# Company and launch remit

PorridgePier runs small paid information services for family carers. Its existing products concern household paperwork, not meals. LadlePath would be a new subscription line with its own profit statement.

The approved opportunity is reducing the unpaid administrative work involved in sharing evening meals for an older relative. Finance approved $105,000 for the first launch, including any annual commercial agreement signed before launch. Two engineers and the named editor are available. Staff are not shared with another launch.

A regional family association can recruit 80 households for an eight-week paid cohort. Consent for time diaries is part of enrollment. Participants continue using their own food suppliers. The association has asked that we make no treatment claims and provide a clear cancellation route.

The ongoing editorial and support model costs $5 per active household per month at the proposed cap. The proposed price can cover those costs without income from the association.
