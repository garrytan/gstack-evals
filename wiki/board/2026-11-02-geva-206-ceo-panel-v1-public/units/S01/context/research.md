# Household interviews

We interviewed 18 coordinators whose families already share meals for an older relative. All keep a short list of accepted meals. Fifteen said choosing dishes takes less than ten minutes a week. The other three use a recurring supermarket meal card and do not want more recipes.

Sixteen spend more than an hour each week finding out which relative will cook on which day. Their longest message threads concern unanswered offers, changes of cook and meals arriving twice. Every interviewed household had at least one uncovered evening in the preceding month despite having groceries in the house.

Participants liked consolidated lists as a convenience. In ranking the tasks they would pay to remove, every participant placed assigning responsibility and confirming delivery above selecting meals. Each participant could use a web link and already paid at least one household bill online. Most preferred email to a new mobile application.
