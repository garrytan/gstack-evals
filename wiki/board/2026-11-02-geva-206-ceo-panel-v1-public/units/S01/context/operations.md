# Available service components

We have licensed 220 household recipes for twelve months at a fixed fee. The license includes ordinary portion scaling and permits replacement of unpopular dishes. An editor can maintain the expected weekly volume within the assigned hours. None of the recipes is presented as a clinical diet.

Payment collection, cancellation and email delivery are supplied by our existing subscription service. No new commercial agreement is needed for those functions. Support can handle 25 additional contacts per week at the cohort size.

The association has agreed to baseline and follow-up time diaries. It will record all coordination time, including calls, shopping arrangements and reminders to relatives. Renewal and editorial costs are available from existing financial reports. Any second cohort needs a new written budget decision after the eight-week results.
