# Northern depot issue

GateWren has used plate-based appointment matching for three years. A recent carrier consolidation increased vehicle substitutions from 6% to 23% of appointments. The booking still exists, but the arriving vehicle differs from the plate captured days earlier. Gate staff send those drivers to the desk to search by booking details. Of 310 observed desk diversions, 294 were resolved by finding a valid booking for the same carrier and a different plate.

The depot group wants fewer desk trips while keeping its identity checks. Drivers and dispatchers do not need different appointment windows or a new booking channel. The current carrier directory records authorized contacts, and all eight depots use it. Their booking volumes total about 480 trucks in the busiest morning period. The proposed reference is an additional lookup key, not evidence of permission to enter on its own.
