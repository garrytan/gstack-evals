# Earlier operational trials

Four of the northern depots used a manual stable-reference process for six weeks before this software project. Staff assigned the existing booking number as the reference, accepted plate changes from the carrier directory contacts, and performed normal document checks at the gate. The trial covered 1,260 bookings, including 290 substitutions and two peak mornings above the projected load of an individual launch depot.

Desk diversions fell to 1.3%, with no identity exceptions and a lower median queue time. Dispatchers at all participating carriers completed the updates through their existing contact channel. The other four depots have the same admission rules and directory, and each has completed a recorded-process rehearsal at its own expected load. Security and operations signed acceptance of the procedure before authorizing construction.

The week-7 all-depot event is the next scheduled group-wide peak morning. It checks deployment coordination and reporting after installation. The carrier groups are already bound by a signed update procedure and have named trained shift contacts. Their participation and ability to make plate changes are established by the earlier trial, not awaiting the group event.
