# Existing service
Cinderbook serves 420 wholesale suppliers. It issues invoices, records delivery and imports a daily bank-credit file. Its batch-matching service already handles the thirty proposed pilot suppliers with 96% automatic matches. Staff review the rest. The batch service has spare capacity for sixty more accounts and no additional license fee.

It can also support a six-week assisted trial of payment promises and dispute ownership. Account staff could maintain the two extra fields in an existing internal table, at a total cost of $4,000. A supplier would receive the same reminder suppression and daily call list planned for the full desk, with more manual work behind the scenes.

Current service agreements allow a separately priced collections add-on. Finance managers can buy it during account reviews, and no customer migration is required.
