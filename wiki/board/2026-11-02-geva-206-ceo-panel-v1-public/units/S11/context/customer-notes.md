# Collections interviews
Nine of twelve interviewed managers said that linking promises and disputes to bank credits would be useful. Three already do this with shared spreadsheets and do not expect to change. The useful accounts described duplicate reminder work and missed promised-payment calls. A two-week staff exercise showed that a combined call queue could save forty minutes per collector per day.

No interview tested a paid purchase. All nine positive responses were collected before the $240 add-on price was selected. The sales team records willingness to pay at that price as an open question. Five suppliers have agreed to a paid six-week assisted trial using the existing service if it is offered this quarter.

The target result is fewer aged balances at the same staffing level. Managers are willing to share aging and staff-time records for evaluation. They do not regard sending more messages as a benefit in itself.
