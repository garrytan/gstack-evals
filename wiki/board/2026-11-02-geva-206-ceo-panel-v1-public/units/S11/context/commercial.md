# Budget and supplier terms
The board approved up to $360,000 for the collections release. That approval permits, but does not require, an eighteen-month matching-service reservation. The quoted $210,000 contract has no early termination, volume reduction or refund provision. Its reserved capacity is equivalent to 150 suppliers. A later start date is available at the same rate for the next three months.

At 100 paying add-on customers, the planned service has positive contribution after partner charges, account support and hosting. The company has funds to cover the quoted term. The commercial question is whether enough existing suppliers will pay the selected add-on price, not whether the service can be financed during launch.

Three engineers are assigned for twelve weeks. The account-management team can support thirty pilots within current staffing. No material approval, bank-access or delivery dependency remains beyond selecting a matching-service arrangement and testing paid uptake.
