# Prior reminder exercise
Last quarter, fourteen suppliers doubled their automatic reminder frequency for six weeks. Delivered reminders per open invoice rose from 1.8 to 3.7. Thirty-day overdue balances rose from 9.4% to 11.2% of receivables, while collector hours remained flat. Buyer replies increasingly concerned disputes that had already been opened with another supplier employee.

The follow-up review found that promises and dispute ownership, not message volume, distinguished accounts that recovered balances. In a small assisted exercise, suppressing disputed reminders and making promised-payment calls reduced aged balances despite sending fewer reminders. Buyer opt-outs stayed below 1% in both groups.

The company objective approved for the next release is a 20% reduction in thirty-day overdue balances with no increase in collector hours. The finance team can produce that measure weekly from existing records.
