# Hearthbatch reporting recovery

## The support target
Hearthbatch manages purchasing for groups of commercial kitchens. Support agents now spend forty hours a month assembling monthly spend evidence for customers. We will reduce that work to ten hours a month by giving kitchen-group finance teams a dedicated BudgetRoom. The first release is included in the current subscription and will serve all thirty groups that request these reports.

The goal is less manual report preparation with no increase in rejected customer audit submissions. We will keep purchasing, supplier payment and recipe costing unchanged. Two engineers and a reporting specialist have eight weeks assigned, within a $76,000 allocation.

## BudgetRoom
The new tab will assemble a monthly package containing the supplier ledger, invoice images, category totals, approval history and a signed snapshot of the period. A finance user can choose a month, download the package and give it to an auditor. The contents will use Hearthbatch's existing category definitions and preserve the customer's account permissions.

We will introduce this as a new reporting destination, separate from Monthpack. Both tabs will remain available. BudgetRoom will use the same customer entitlement and offer the same downloadable archive format, with no added storage fee. The release does not add new countries, reporting periods, evidence types or accounting treatments.

The specialist will create the package through the new service and show finance teams how to repeat the process. Account managers can enable it under existing contracts. We will invite the thirty groups in their regular finance review, with a recorded walkthrough and a scheduled first download.

## Provider and operating approach
We will use the Quernpath document service to assemble and sign the package. Its quoted per-package fee fits our existing subscription margin. Customer invoice data will stay in the approved region, and the service will receive only the fields already permitted by our processing agreement. Hearthbatch will retain its own source records and can regenerate historical reports.

Customers will not need a Quernpath account. The reporting specialist will handle operational questions through the existing support queue. We will keep the old reporting functions available during rollout, and a group may continue requesting help on a monthly basis while it learns the new tab.

## Work calendar
Weeks one and two build the report shell and map data fields. Weeks three through five implement the archive, snapshot signature and account controls. Weeks six and seven finish period selection, support tools and customer documentation.

The separately funded Monthpack signing migration runs in parallel through week seven and cuts over in week nine. Its customer-download entitlement will join the same week-eight review.

In week eight we will present the complete service to Quernpath's commercial team for the embedded-service entitlement review and run the release trial. We will reserve the final two days for changes resulting from that review. Customer activation follows the review's completion. No paid release will use a package lacking the required entitlement.

## Evidence for continuing
For three reporting cycles, we will log support preparation minutes for the fixed thirty-group cohort and count auditor rejections attributable to incomplete Hearthbatch evidence. Customer finance leads will confirm that the package meets their existing submission requirements. We will compare the results with the prior three cycles rather than count visits to the new tab.

We will continue broad rollout only if preparation drops to ten hours or less each month and no new evidence-related rejection occurs. Account managers can restore assisted reports immediately for any affected group. The next budget review will use saved labor, total provider fees and customer renewal results, with no requirement to retire the old reporting destination.
