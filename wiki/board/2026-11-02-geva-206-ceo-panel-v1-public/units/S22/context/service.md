# Current reporting
Hearthbatch serves commercial kitchen groups. Thirty finance teams request a monthly evidence package through support, consuming forty staff hours each month. Their requested contents are supplier ledger, invoice images, category totals, approval history and a signed monthly snapshot. All use the same archive format and category definitions.

Monthpack, already included in their subscriptions, supplies exactly that package from the finance menu. All thirty have permission to use it, with no added charge or country restriction. During observed sessions, finance users at all thirty groups downloaded complete packages from Monthpack. Auditors accepted every sample. Download took under four minutes and required no support preparation.

Finance teams asked for no additional evidence, retention change or different report layout. Customers can continue using Monthpack indefinitely under the current agreement.
