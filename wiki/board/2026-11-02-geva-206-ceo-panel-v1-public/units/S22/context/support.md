# Support records
The monthly preparation requests are spread across all thirty kitchen groups. Agents have traditionally assembled the reports and emailed them rather than walking customers through Monthpack. The recent observation sessions were the first time several finance users had been shown the existing download. Existing account reviews can include a scheduled reporting walkthrough at no extra staffing cost.

There have been no report rejections from Monthpack in the last twelve months. The support objective is to reduce preparation work, not increase the number of reporting destinations. Customers want to keep the existing purchasing and payment workflow.

Finance managers can approve new reporting access through their current contracts. They can attend a first-download session. No external procurement, training certification or change of accounting provider is needed to use either reporting destination.
