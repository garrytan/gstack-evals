# Opening customer terms
Elmvein Housing signed a twelve-month agreement with Brambleturn for at least 240 vacant-home resets, including sixty in the first twelve operating weeks. It pays $1,400 per standard reset and funds a rolling deposit sufficient for booked trade work. The agreement covers two neighboring districts and three standard home sizes.

The purchase condition is a complete package: lock replacement, deep cleaning, minor patching, waste removal and meter readings. Elmvein will not buy a partial package or a standalone calendar. Its existing bundled provider can be replaced only when one incoming provider accepts the complete ready date and consolidated invoice. The five tasks are part of the signed acceptance checklist, not suggested future additions.

Structural repairs and occupied properties remain with Elmvein. It can reduce weekly bookings by agreement if readiness falls below target. It has supplied sample vacancy data and approved the standard evidence packet.
