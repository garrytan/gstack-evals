# Reserved delivery
Brambleturn has signed service schedules with a primary and a backup provider for each trade. Their named teams have capacity for ten resets per week across the two districts. Six weekly slots are reserved for the opening service; the rest provide cover for rework and disruption. Required trade qualifications and insurance are current and were checked before the customer agreement.

Partners receive staged payment from the customer deposit. Each accepts mobile web job lists and the agreed evidence checklist. No partner is being asked to recruit before opening. Their cancellation fees and maximum patching allowances appear in the customer schedule.

The part-time operations coordinator has twenty-five hours a week. Rehearsal estimates allow two hours per reset plus five weekly coordination hours at the six-reset cap. Backup coverage is contracted for coordinator absence and included in the operating allowance.
