# Startup and service budget
The $120,000 startup allocation is funded by the founders. It covers three engineers for ten weeks, rehearsals, legal setup and contingency. They do not need another customer, loan or annual supplier prepayment to complete the opening release. Customer deposits are ring-fenced for booked reset work and are not counted as software funding.

The measured $960 per-reset operating allowance includes all five trades, coordination, backup cover and expected rework. The $1,400 contract price leaves $440 contribution before fixed company overhead. Annual guaranteed volume supports the $54,000 fixed overhead budget with room for ordinary variance. The one-time software build is covered by startup funds.

The team estimates the fixed-menu workspace at ten weeks. Adding more trades, variable workflow design or a third district is outside that estimate. The customer has accepted the six-per-week booking cap and the staged rehearsal before normal operation.
