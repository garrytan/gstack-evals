# Vacancy workflow
Observation of twenty recent resets found that all five standard tasks occurred at every property. Lock replacement must precede trade access; cleaning follows patching and waste collection. Meter readings accompany final handover. Missing evidence from any track prevents the operator from assigning a new tenancy date.

Elmvein currently spends four coordinator hours per home chasing separate updates. Its procurement team wants one accountable ready date and accepts a fixed service menu. It does not need routing optimization, tenant messaging, custom workflow design or a new rent ledger.

Two empty properties are available for rehearsals in week six. Five paid resets can run before full opening. Elmvein's representative can review sample completion packets within one working day. No additional departmental approval is needed once the package meets the signed checklist.
