# Brambleturn opening release

## The first contract
Brambleturn is a new apartment-reset service with a shared software workspace for housing operators and local trade teams. We will open with Elmvein Housing and deliver sixty vacant-home resets over the first twelve operating weeks. The operator will receive one confirmed ready date, one evidence package and one invoice for each home.

Our goal is to fulfill that opening contract with at least 95% of homes ready by their agreed date and no missing completion evidence. The first release is not a general marketplace. It will serve one operator, two neighboring districts and a fixed service menu. The approved startup allocation is $120,000, separate from customer deposits that cover service costs.

## Everything included at opening
The first release will include five service tracks: lock replacement, deep cleaning, minor wall patching, waste removal and meter readings. The workspace will schedule every track, collect its completion evidence and prevent a home being marked ready until all five are signed off. All five tracks are required for the first customer opening. We will not release a cleaning-only or scheduling-only version first.

The operator will upload a vacancy list and select one of three standard home sizes. Brambleturn will return a ready date and arrange the work. Each trade partner gets its own job list and evidence checklist. The operator can see delays and approve a changed date. A final packet will contain completion photos, lock handover, disposal receipt and meter readings, with a consolidated invoice.

## Boundaries
We will not handle structural repairs, occupied homes, emergency callouts or resident rent collection. A home outside the agreed patching allowance returns to the operator's existing repairs team. The workspace will support a fixed five-track sequence with manual coordinator intervention, not a configurable workflow builder. Partners will use mobile web pages rather than separate native applications.

Home access will follow the operator's current key-handover protocol. The operator retains the vacancy record and can export the full job history. A cancelled job follows the signed service schedule, including any documented partner cancellation charge. No supplier will be required to hold unpaid capacity beyond its contracted allocation.

## Preparation and launch
Three engineers have ten weeks to build intake, partner lists, evidence capture, exception handling and consolidated billing. The operations coordinator will rehearse the five-track sequence with empty test properties in week six. Weeks eight and nine are reserved for five paid rehearsal resets. Week ten opens the planned customer schedule only after those homes meet the agreed acceptance checklist.

The coordinator owns the complete ready date. Each trade partner owns its staffing and task completion. We will book no more than six resets per week in the opening period. A second partner in each trade can cover the reserved contingency capacity under the same schedule.

## Service and financial review
The operator will check readiness, evidence completeness and rework every week. We will compare actual partner charges and coordinator time with the $960 operating allowance per reset against a $1,400 selling price. Extra work needs an operator-approved change order and does not silently enter the standard package.

We will add no district or service track during the first twelve weeks. A second operator requires the ready-date target, complete evidence for every reset and positive contribution after rework and coordination. If the opening service falls below those limits, we will reduce weekly bookings with the customer's agreement rather than promise capacity we have not reserved.
