# Retail accounts

LoopCrate has 310 shop accounts in one city. Forty central-district shops have asked for consolidated billing and flexible booking because managers dislike approving each small invoice. Those shops already know the collection process and can adopt a membership through their current account manager. The current contract permits a subscription addendum without a new procurement process.

Observed use among these forty shops ranges from 16 to 24 collections per month, averaging 20. Each visit typically retrieves only two or three crates. Managers want to keep that pattern rather than store a month's crates for a single visit. All forty signed a nonbinding interest form at the proposed membership price. No shop offered a separate access fee or minimum purchase commitment.

Shops rate collection reliability highly. Missed pickups are below 1.5%. The retention issue is invoice approval work, not route availability, labels, or driver conduct. Membership is a new commercial offer; no subscription billing function exists today.
