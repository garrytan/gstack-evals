# Recurring economics and staffing

Each central-district collection creates $3.10 in unavoidable incremental driver pay, vehicle charge, and crate handling. This is the marginal cost after existing route overhead is paid. The operations team measured it over three months and expects the same rate across 16 to 24 visits per shop. Combining visits from different shops on a route is already reflected in that figure. The cost does not fall with membership enrollment.

Membership payment and account service add $1.20 per shop each month. LoopCrate earns no packaging margin, resale income, or other revenue from collected crates. No sponsor or other business line will fund membership losses. The launch allocation covers construction only, and finance requires the ongoing membership to pay its recurring costs from membership receipts.

There are 80 spare collection slots each weekday. Forty shops at current use would consume about 40 daily slots. Drivers and vehicles are secured for the whole pilot. More frequent use is possible within the physical capacity limit, so the cost forecast does not depend on a staffing shortage.
