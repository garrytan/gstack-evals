# Placement administration study

Fieldquill is a new company formed by three people who previously administered vocational placements. The research covered eight training providers, 46 learners and 19 workplace supervisors. The providers collectively manage 14,800 placements each year. Six provider directors have budgeted between $18,000 and $35,000 annually for a cohort administration service.

The directors want to replace spreadsheet chasing. They need a single view of missing supervisor checks, bulk reminders and an administrator who can correct placement assignments. Each director controls the records policy and the software purchase. Their purchasing process accepts a standard annual agreement and a four-week evaluation. They have no requirement for mentoring or public project displays.

Learners already receive a free calendar and personal evidence template from their provider. Forty-one of the 46 said those personal materials were sufficient. Five wanted a nicer portable evidence book, but none agreed to pay the proposed $12 monthly price in the price-choice exercise. A learner cannot replace the provider's central reporting process by inviting a supervisor into a personal account.
