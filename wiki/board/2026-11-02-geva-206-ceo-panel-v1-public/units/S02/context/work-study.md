# Observations from placements

Administrators spend a median of nine hours per cohort each week locating incomplete sign-offs. Supervisors usually complete a check within a day once the correct request reaches them. The delay comes from staff discovering that requests were never assigned or were attached to the wrong placement. Learners generally submit their weekly evidence on time.

A test with a shared cohort board cut administration time to three hours per week. The test used the same evidence templates as before. Provider directors said calendar, sign-off collection, a cohort board and export would be sufficient to purchase. They did not request a change to course content.

Student society officers offered access to their mailing lists, with normal consent and unsubscribe rules. Those lists reach learners directly, not provider purchasing staff. The officers will distribute either a free or a paid offer and make no claim about its usefulness. Alumni currently arrange mentoring through their societies. None of the eight providers makes mentoring or public exhibitions part of placement completion.
