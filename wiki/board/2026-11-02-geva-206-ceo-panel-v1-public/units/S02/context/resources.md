# Funding and estimates

The approved product budget is $420,000 for seven months, including a three-person engineering team, a designer and part-time operations. The team estimated ten weeks for calendars, assignment management, sign-off collection and evidence exports. Adding an institution account and the cohort board would take a further three weeks using the same data model.

The alumni exhibition is estimated at seven additional weeks. Mentor listing, booking, payments and cancellation administration add ten weeks. These estimates include testing and exclude no named vendor dependency. Both additions can be released separately after the placement service. There are no signed orders tying either addition to a placement purchase.

Hosted file storage, payment processing and support costs fit either a provider subscription or the proposed learner price at the expected volume. Funding does not depend on marketplace commission revenue. The investor agreement permits a smaller first release and keeps unused funds available for later work.
