# Fieldquill launch proposal

## What we will sell
Fieldquill will sell a personal practicum organizer for learners entering supervised work placements. Our first-year goal is $180,000 in recurring revenue from the placement-administration market. The product will put the placement calendar, supervisor sign-offs and a portable evidence book in one place. We will keep the offer narrow to placements that last between six and sixteen weeks.

## First customers
The first release will serve individual learners rather than training providers. Learners will buy a $12 monthly subscription, invite a supervisor and keep their records after graduation. We will not include a provider account, central cohort administration or institutional purchasing in this release. Student societies at the eight research sites have agreed to circulate an invitation. The landing page will show a complete sample placement and the exact subscription price before signup.

The subscription will include one active placement and unlimited retained evidence books. A learner can cancel monthly and export a readable copy. Supervisors participate without paying. We will charge only once a learner has created a placement and accepted the supervisor invitation.

## Release contents
The initial delivery will include calendars, sign-off requests, an evidence book, an alumni project exhibition and a mentor-session marketplace. All five areas will ship together at the end of month seven. The exhibition will support themed collections and invited public viewers. The marketplace will let alumni list paid mentoring appointments, take payment and manage cancellations. We will accept no subscription payments until the full release is available.

The team will use hosted identity and payment services. We will not build a course library, placement discovery service or employer recruitment board. Files will have clear ownership and export controls, and supervisors will see only placements to which they are invited.

## Delivery and resources
Three engineers and one designer have seven months of approved funding. The placement organizer takes the first ten weeks. The exhibition and mentoring work follow, with integration and accessibility testing in the final month. A part-time operations lead will handle mentor verification using a capped first group of twenty mentors. Payments cover mentor fees and processing, with a separate ten percent platform charge.

## Decision at month nine
We will measure paid subscriptions, net recurring revenue, eight-week retention and completed supervisor sign-offs. The release must reach 1,250 paid subscriptions by month nine, with at least 70 percent of active placements obtaining all required sign-offs. We will not broaden the placement types until retention and sign-off rates clear those thresholds. Support hours and refund rates will be reviewed weekly. If payment conversion falls below four percent, we will stop acquisition spending and interview departing learners before changing the offer.
