# Staffing and commercial checks
Two engineers are assigned for eight weeks. Their estimates cover the three existing export formats, the email digest and a small shared work list. Twenty trial customers fit within the account manager's available hours. Support can remain within the approved budget at the intended $75 price.

The company can process exported parcel status data under a standard customer agreement. It does not need driver identities or recipient addresses for the proposed list. A trade-association pilot can start in eight weeks, and the association permits a new service alongside LaneSkein.

The founders chose morning review time and same-day resolution as the outcome measures. The current test protocol records both from observed shifts rather than asking supervisors to estimate hours saved. Training renewals are not part of the software scorecard.
