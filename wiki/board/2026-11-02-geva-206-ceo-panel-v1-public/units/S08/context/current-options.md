# Tools available to the cohort
LaneSkein's standard edition includes a feature called Morning List. All twenty research depots have it enabled at no added charge. It accepts a selected local delivery hour, emails late collections, unassigned parcels and open claims, and provides source links and owner assignment. The weekly report shows resolution time. Records can be exported without a fee.

In a supervised session, all twenty supervisors opened Morning List with their normal account. Sixteen completed the full morning review in twelve to fifteen minutes. Four took seventeen minutes while learning the owner field. The vendor provides onboarding at no charge and supports all three formats in this cohort.

Managers reported no need for different data retention, branding, ownership or control. They can keep using Morning List under their current contracts whether or not they buy another tool.
