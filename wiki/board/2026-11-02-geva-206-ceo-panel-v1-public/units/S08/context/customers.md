# Supervisor fieldwork
Twenty independent depots participated in the discovery work. Supervisors spend a median forty minutes compiling morning exceptions from carrier records. The important categories are late collections, unassigned parcels and open carrier claims. A clear owner and a link back to the original record are sufficient for their daily review.

Every depot uses the standard edition of LaneSkein, a carrier-management service. The subscription is renewed by the depot manager. Customers are permitted to export their records and use other software. Supervisors do not need a new routing system, stock system or driver app.

The trade association has offered twenty opt-in trial invitations through its vendor program. Managers can authorize monthly subscriptions without central procurement. All twenty have suitable email access and can upload the existing export.
