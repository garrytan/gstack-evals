# Current business
The company publishes depot-supervisor courses and sells them through a training distributor. The line earns $90,000 of contribution each quarter after instructor and account-management costs. Renewal orders for the next two quarters are signed. Course maintenance takes one curriculum contractor two days a month. The account manager has eight hours a week available for another offer without closing the line.

The software project has an approved $82,000 budget held in cash. That money does not depend on selling the training catalog. The founders have no debt covenant, investor condition or distributor deadline requiring a sale or a shutdown. The catalog has been updated this year and can remain on sale under the present agreement.

The distributor's purchase draft offers $40,000 for permanent exclusive rights and a five-year restriction on the company selling similar training. It contains no repurchase clause or license back.
