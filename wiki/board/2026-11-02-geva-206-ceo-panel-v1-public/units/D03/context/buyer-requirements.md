# Cooperative operating rules

The six cooperatives operate fourteen stores under a shared data policy. Store computers must remain disconnected from outside services during price processing, including overnight. The policy covers product names and prices as well as customer data. A managed internet connection, overnight upload, or remote rendering request does not satisfy their signed purchase conditions. Their internal auditors will test outbound traffic before accepting the product.

The cooperatives currently prepare labels by hand, taking an average of 75 minutes each evening. Managers confirmed that a local watched-folder process fits their stock exports and staff routines. Their technicians can install signed packages and provide local printer access. They have already approved the new monthly price and reserved installation dates. The buying group requires no additional procurement stage after the pilot acceptance tests.

Stores change between 80 and 240 labels a night, well within a single computer's measured local rendering capacity. They want the same label content and paper size as the hosted edition, not a redesigned retail labeling system.
