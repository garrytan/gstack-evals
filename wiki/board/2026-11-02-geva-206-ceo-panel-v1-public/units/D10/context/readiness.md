# Next-group arrangements

Six training providers have signed invitations and data-handling terms for up to 2,000 candidates. Four participated in the manual pilot and two serve the same occupational segments. The providers will handle invitations through their current learner contact channels. Employers receive no unsolicited sales message when a candidate shares a record; they must request contact.

The eight-week engineering estimate covers sharing, withdrawal, verified-item labels and export, using the existing document service. Verification staff have 320 reserved productive hours for the first group against an estimated 240 hours at pilot handling times. The sales team has room for twice the pilot demonstration rate. No hire, provider approval or purchasing permission remains to be secured before launch.

Support and storage for inactive candidate records fit the approved budget even if acquisition is stopped. The gate uses retained paid employers and contribution after all candidate-service costs. Provider agreements permit a capped group and do not require wider rollout. Existing employer customers will not lose features or capacity when the addition opens.
