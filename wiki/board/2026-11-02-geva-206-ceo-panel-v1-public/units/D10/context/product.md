# Current product and buyers

Fenncross provides applicant tracking, document collection and hiring administration for small industrial employers. Employers purchase annual subscriptions through an owner or authorized operations manager. Candidates do not control those budgets. Training providers already refer candidates and employers to Fenncross and have signed participation terms for the portable-record program.

Current customers can collect documents inside a single application, but a candidate cannot carry a verified record to a different employer. Providers report repeated requests for the same training evidence when a worker applies again elsewhere. The proposed portable record adds candidate-controlled reuse across employers rather than reproducing an existing account feature.

The company has assigned $126,000 to the first release and its first year of support. Its objective is profitable employer acquisition, not direct payment from workers. The present sales team can process employer referrals under existing pricing. The employer service remains usable without the portable record, and every candidate can export their own material.
