# Fenncross portable work record

## Why this addition
Fenncross sells hiring administration to small industrial employers. We will add a free portable work record for candidates, with the aim of bringing qualified new employers into the paid product. The record will hold candidate-approved training evidence and verified prior assignments. A candidate can share it with any employer, including one that does not yet buy Fenncross.

The first-year business target is forty new paid employer accounts acquired through shared records, with acquisition cost below $1,100 per account. We will not count candidate registrations as revenue or make the candidate tool a paid consumer business. Current employer customers keep their existing pricing and workflows.

## Candidate-first release
The launch campaign will address candidates rather than employer purchasing managers. Candidates will create and share their record for free. We will distribute invitations through the training providers already participating in our referral program. The first release will not include an employer cold-outreach campaign.

A receiving manager can inspect a shared record without buying anything. The page will offer a separately scheduled demonstration for employers that want ongoing hiring administration. An employer's authorized purchasing manager will then use the existing quote and contract process. A sales specialist will handle that transition; candidates will never be asked to approve or pay for an employer account.

## What the record includes
The addition will support approved document uploads, assignment confirmations and time-limited sharing links. Candidates will control each share and can withdraw access. The record will distinguish candidate statements from verified items. Employers will retain responsibility for checking role-specific requirements. We will not rank candidates, sell their contact details or promise that a shared record alone establishes eligibility for a role.

## Delivery and service limits
Three engineers and a designer will deliver the record in eight weeks, using the current document store and consent system. The first group is capped at 2,000 candidates from six training providers. Existing verification staff have reserved time for the group's expected review volume. The $126,000 build and first-year support budget is approved. We will add no new record types during the first measurement period.

## What would earn expansion
We will retain a comparison group using current provider referrals and count signed, paying employer accounts after their first ninety days. Expansion requires at least twenty paid employers per thousand active candidates, positive expected employer contribution after acquisition and verification costs, and no rise in candidate privacy complaints. If that gate fails, we will pause acquisition while keeping existing record access and exports available. A larger candidate audience is not itself a reason to increase spending.
