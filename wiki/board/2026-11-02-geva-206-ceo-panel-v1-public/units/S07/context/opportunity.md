# Distributor opportunity

DockLark is a new company with $90,000 in founder funding. Two regional grocery distributors supplied data from 1,800 evening stops. Failed refrigerated handoffs cost them return transport and spoilage, averaging $46 each. Both operating directors want that number reduced and are willing to lend staff to a limited trial.

The proposed price would cover messaging, event licensing, and an operations allocation at ten depots. Existing contracts leave food custody and delivery execution with the distributors. Event data may be used for arrival notices under a signed standard license. Neither carrier integration nor message delivery requires a new commercial permission.

The purchasing actor is the distributor's finance director. Depot managers can provide records and volunteer for research but cannot approve a new recurring supplier. Neither finance director has reviewed a price or allocated a budget for DockLark. Both said they might instead pay for a different dispatch tool already used elsewhere in their company.
