# Observed evening routes

Researchers followed 210 stops and matched them to driver logs. Of the 29 failed handoffs, 26 occurred after the shop's receiving staff had left. These shops end paid receiving shifts at 17:00. Drivers reach them between 17:35 and 18:20 because a wholesale loading agreement releases the refrigerated vehicles at 17:10. The agreement remains fixed for the next nine months.

Managers already receive accurate arrival estimates from the drivers' dispatchers. In all 26 cases they knew the arrival time before leaving. No employee remained on site, and managers cannot extend paid shifts from their current store budgets. The owners interviewed would consider a funded evening holding service, but they will not return personally to receive a single delivery.

Three other failures involved damaged seals and are handled under an existing quality program. Daytime stores rarely miss deliveries, even where dispatchers send only one notice. Accurate contact records still help the desk close out exceptions and avoid calling former employees.
